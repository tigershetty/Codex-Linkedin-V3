## Hook options

Write ten genuinely different entrances. Do not generate ten paraphrases of one formula.

1. **Witnessed scene:** a moment Tiger can credibly place the reader inside.
2. **Downstream consequence:** the cost, delay, service failure, career limit, or decision error.
3. **Meeting quote:** language a practitioner would actually hear.
4. **Professional identity:** help the reader locate themselves or their team.
5. **Reversal:** a familiar belief that fails under a specific condition.
6. **Proof-led:** a verified count, experiment, result, or repeated pattern.
7. **Boundary:** distinguish two concepts people incorrectly collapse.
8. **Before / after decision:** what changes when one input moves.
9. **Recognizable case:** company, project, role, or operating event with a source.
10. **Unresolved question:** a tension with real competing answers.

For every option, note the receipt or evidence it uses. Reject hooks that require an unsupported statistic, invented anecdote, or false certainty.

## Caption path

Use the shortest path that earns the idea. A flagship may need 300-450 words; a sharp field note may need fewer. Length does not prove value.

1. Open with the selected tension.
2. Show where the insight came from.
3. Name the operating or career consequence.
4. Explain the minimum mechanism needed to support Tiger's belief.
5. State the proof boundary or competing trade-off.
6. Give the reader one reusable decision lens, test, or next action.
7. End with a question that invites examples, disagreement, or a real choice.
