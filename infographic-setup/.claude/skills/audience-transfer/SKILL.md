# Audience Transfer

Measure the adaptation, not the worth of its saved references.

## Capture

At 24 hours, 7 days, and 28 days, record channel-native metrics, content package IDs, reference
atoms, claim mode, resource use, and one next adaptation. Keep LinkedIn, Substack and website
metrics separate.

For high-reach LinkedIn posts, record a small manual sample of visible followers and engagers with
role/relevance notes. It is diagnostic only: it must not automatically block broad topics or formats.

## Learn monthly

Compare matched-age and matched-format posts. Report raw outcomes and rates separately. Identify
which attention, comprehension, utility and bridge atoms transferred, which did not, and which
creative bets deserve another test. Do not use a universal score or downgrade a saved source because
one adaptation underperformed.
