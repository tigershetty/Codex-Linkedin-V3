# Opportunity Desk

Build opportunities from a real reader tension, not a topic calendar or generic format.

## Inputs

Use one or both lanes:

- a saved-post mechanism paired with a real planning, purchasing, transformation, career, or industry tension;
- a verified timely signal when it changes a real professional decision.

Broad reach is allowed. Name the likely broad reader and the bridge into practitioner-grade value.

## Output

Prepare a dossier and concept board containing:

1. primary reader, recognisable work or career moment, and care statement;
2. reader response: recognition, understanding, practical use, or deliberate intrigue;
3. proportionate evidence and claim boundary;
4. two to four reviewed reference atoms for a standard post, or a declared flagship query;
5. three compactly different creative routes;
6. resource decision: none, template, playbook, or tool.

Build a resource only when the tension has repeated or enduring demand. Do not use a resource as a
lead-capture prop.
