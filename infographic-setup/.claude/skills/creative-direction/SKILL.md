# Creative Direction

Use `references/creative-review/signature-system-v1.md` as the active visual guardrail. No visual
family is a default.

## Route design

Create three routes that differ in reader moment, opening move, visual primitive, useful keep, and
reason to kill—not merely their title, colour or icon.

For each route declare:

- reader response and visible proof;
- selected visual family;
- eye path and feed-size hierarchy;
- what the reader keeps or feels;
- reference atoms transformed and anti-copy boundary;
- proportionate claim support;
- whether motion would clarify sequence, comparison, or consequence.

Tiger selects, kills, or redirects a route before finished production. A beautiful visual may earn
attention, but it still needs a human or operational tension; do not build decorative AI spectacle.
