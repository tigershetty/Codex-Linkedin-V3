# GIF Storyboard

Animate the approved argument; do not redesign it.

## Confirm eligibility

1. Read `references/motion-engine-v1.md`, `reference-bundle.json`, `recombination-brief.md`, and the
   approved `visual-output-review.md`.
2. Confirm `visual.png` is explicitly approved.
3. State what motion adds: sequence, comparison, change, calculation, traversal, or emphasis.
4. Record a still-only exception when motion adds no comprehension or would weaken the design.

Do not force motion by cadence, lane, renderer, or style. A spreadsheet, illustrated scene, chart,
document, interface, or HTML render may all be eligible.

## Preserve the still

- Start and end with the approved composition intact.
- Reuse its content, hierarchy, brand, numbers, labels, logos, and reading route.
- Add no unsupported facts, decorative subplots, new CTA, or alternate visual thesis.
- Keep exact text and data deterministic.
- Match the opening and closing lossless frames to the approved still.

## Build the motion argument

Inventory the semantic components, then choose one spine that fits the selected direction. Examples:

- reveal steps in decision order;
- move a token through a process or network;
- assemble inputs into a formula and result;
- compare states before resolving the decisive difference;
- expose a bottleneck, exception, or constraint;
- build a hierarchy before landing on the useful action.

Write a beat table with `time | component | action | purpose | completion state`. Let timing follow
the amount of information rather than a fixed duration. Hold the complete final frame long enough to
read.

## Implement and verify

Use the motion surface selected by Motion Engine v1. Keep timelines seek-safe and deterministic when
using the in-repo renderer. Render MP4 and GIF only when both are useful.

Verify:

- the argument is easier to follow than the still alone;
- every value and label matches the approved source;
- the loop does not hide the payoff;
- the first and final complete frames match `visual.png`;
- mobile-scale text remains legible;
- no new claim escaped the support ledger.

Save the storyboard, implementation source, rendered files, and motion QA beside the content package.
