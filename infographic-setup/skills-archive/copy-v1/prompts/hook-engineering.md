# Hook Engineering — Infographic Content Engine v1

The infographic title is the LinkedIn thumbnail headline. At 450px wide in the feed, it has approximately 0.3 seconds to earn a tap. This is the single highest-leverage copy decision in the entire pipeline.

---

## The Three Hook Types

### Type A: Stakes-Led
**Formula**: [Specific consequence] + [Why it's worse than they think]
**Best for**: Topics where the cost of inaction is surprising or underestimated
**Example**: "73% of Nearshoring Math Is Wrong. Here's the Real Number."
**Why it works**: VPs who have done nearshoring math feel targeted. VPs who haven't feel urgency.

### Type B: Stat-Led
**Formula**: [Surprising number] + [Contrarian claim]
**Best for**: Topics with a single dominant data point that challenges conventional wisdom
**Example**: "12×: Why GLP-1 Is the Unexpected Cold Chain Crisis"
**Why it works**: The number creates specificity + curiosity gap simultaneously.

### Type C: Contrarian
**Formula**: [Wrong assumption most VPs have] stated as a direct challenge
**Best for**: Topics where the audience's current behavior is the problem
**Example**: "Your Supply Chain Is Not Resilient. It's Brittle by Design."
**Why it works**: Triggers cognitive dissonance. Forces the reader to either defend themselves or engage.

---

## Scoring Matrix

Score each variant on 5 criteria (1 point each). Threshold: 3/5 minimum.

| Criterion | What it measures | Fails | Passes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Specificity** | Contains a number or named entity | "Most supply chains struggle..." | "73% of teams underestimate..." |
| **Curiosity Gap** | Creates a knowledge gap to close | "Important supply chain topic" | "Here's why it's actually worse" |
| **Stakes** | Names a VP-relevant consequence | "Improve your operations" | "Save $240M in write-downs" |
| **Contrarian Signal** | Challenges current belief | "Best practices in SCM" | "The math says the opposite" |
| **Thumbnail Readability** | ≤8 words, ALL CAPS at 450px | "A Comprehensive Guide to Understanding Supply Chain Disruption" | "73% OF NEARSHORING MATH IS WRONG" |

---

## Infographic Title vs. Caption Hook

These are DIFFERENT elements — do not confuse them.

| Element | Location | Max Length | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Infographic Title** | Zone T (Title Zone) embedded in image | ≤8 words | The thumbnail that earns the tap |
| **Caption Hook (A section)** | First line of LinkedIn post caption | ≤140 chars | The first sentence that earns the read |

The **Caption Hook** is an expansion of the selected Title variant. It adds the missing number or context that the 8-word title couldn't contain.

**Example**:
- Title: "73% OF NEARSHORING MATH IS WRONG"
- Caption Hook: "73% of CFOs underestimate nearshoring total landed cost by $28-47 per unit. The math changed in Q4 2024."

---

## Portrait Readability Rules (Single-Image Format)

Unlike a carousel where the headline fills a full slide, the infographic title competes with 5-8 other zones for visual space.

- **Word count**: ≤8 words is non-negotiable. Test at 6 words first.
- **Case**: ALL CAPS in the image. Title case in caption only.
- **Numbers**: Put the number FIRST or SECOND. "73%: WHY YOUR NEARSHORING MATH IS WRONG" > "WHY YOUR NEARSHORING MATH IS 73% WRONG"
- **Company names**: Use them. "MAERSK", "NIKE", "AMAZON" are scroll-stoppers that generic topics are not.
- **Colon structure**: Works well. "MAERSK'S BET: THE $7.8B PIVOT THAT CHANGED SHIPPING"

---

## Regeneration Rules

If a variant scores ≤2/5:
1. Identify which criteria it fails
2. Rewrite with those specific criteria in mind
3. Score again
4. Do not present a variant with score ≤2

If all 3 variants score ≤2/5: The topic itself may have a hook problem. Return to research.md Hook Candidates and use a different starting point.

## Examples by Topic Type

### Company Strategic Pivot
- A: "MAERSK SOLD 200 SHIPS. THE REASON IS A $7.8B BET." (4/5)
- B: "$7.8B: MAERSK'S PIVOT FROM SHIPPING TO LOGISTICS" (3/5)
- C: "THE WORLD'S SECOND-LARGEST SHIPPING LINE IS EXITING SHIPPING" (4/5)

### Data / Statistical
- A: "73% OF NEARSHORING MATH UNDERESTIMATES COST BY $28 PER UNIT" (5/5)
- B: "$28/UNIT: THE NEARSHORING MATH MOST CFOS GET WRONG" (4/5)
- C: "YOUR NEARSHORING CASE IS BUILT ON OUTDATED ASSUMPTIONS" (3/5)

### Mechanism / Root Cause
- A: "THE BULLWHIP EFFECT COST $240M IN Q1. HERE'S THE MECHANISM" (4/5)
- B: "4×: HOW DEMAND SIGNAL DISTORTION BECOMES INVENTORY CRISIS" (4/5)
- C: "YOU'RE NOT MANAGING DEMAND. YOU'RE MANAGING NOISE." (4/5)

### Framework / Strategy
- A: "TWO SUPPLY CHAIN STRATEGIES. ONE 4× MARGIN DIFFERENCE." (4/5)
- B: "4×: THE MARGIN GAP BETWEEN DIGITAL AND ANALOG SCM" (3/5)
- C: "MOST S&OP PROCESSES ARE SOLVING THE WRONG PROBLEM" (3/5)
