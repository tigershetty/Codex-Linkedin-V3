 HISTORICAL V5 NATIVE SCENE BLOCK  (not a standard-post default)
------------------------------------------------------------------
Historical example: create one original, image-engine-native Shetty's Desk Operating Studio
**Instrumented Operating Explanation** for {reader and live work moment}.
Choose one coherent operating system: an instrumented decision board, causal
operating atlas, paired-state rig, or another tangible/diagrammatic mechanism
that makes the inputs, causal path, decision boundary, and {one decision rule
or mental object} legible. Use a bespoke **Commitment Object** only whenever
the story has a point of no return: a physical gate, fence, lock, timing rig,
release carriage, route, or boundary where competing pressures act on the
same mechanism. The reader must understand the tension at feed size and keep
the rule at closer look.

Use a luminous white or ice-blue operating world, azure-to-navy systems,
eco-green only for the protected or recommended path, and coral only for
real risk, friction, or exception. Use tactile studio realism: brushed
aluminium, cobalt anodising, clear acrylic, engineering tolerances, and
purposeful depth. This is a premium operating instrument, not stock
logistics photography. Keep on-image words short and engraved, printed, or
otherwise integral to the physical scene.

Render the scene, hierarchy, and typography as one final image. No HTML,
SVG, cards, dashboard mosaic, pasted title bar, footer, watermark, or
model-redrawn logo overlay. Do not invent numbers, outcomes, or company
proof.

For standard native scenes, avoid generic warehouse scenery, a generic SaaS
or app interface, flat graphic tiles, disconnected decorative props, a
headline that has to explain a failed mechanism, or photorealism that feels
like stock logistics photography.

------------------------------------------------------------------
 PRECISION-ARTIFACT STYLE BLOCK  (only for an explicit carousel / exact card)
------------------------------------------------------------------
Flat-design isometric 2.5D vector infographic illustration in the
"Shetty's Desk" brand style. Smooth blue gradients (azure #2798FB
through royal #247BE1 to deep navy #1939A5), rounded geometric shapes,
soft diffused long shadows, subtle glows, clean editorial composition
on a pure white background. A signature green eco-gradient (mint
#8FF3CC to emerald #38E6A6) marks the positive / progress / highlighted
element; coral-pink #E27199 marks any negative / warning element, used
sparingly. Modern, optimistic, science-led, tech-forward. Crisp vector
look — NOT photorealistic, no 3D render, no hard black outlines, no
sketch texture, no dark backgrounds.

------------------------------------------------------------------
 PRECISION-ARTIFACT AVOID LIST  (append only to a carousel / exact-card prompt)
------------------------------------------------------------------
Avoid: photorealism, 3D renders, hard black outlines, sketch/hand-drawn
texture, dark or busy backgrounds, clutter, harsh or black drop shadows,
muddy gradients, and using green for anything negative.

 MODULE TEMPLATES  (precision artifact / carousel only)
------------------------------------------------------------------
HERO SCENE
  A single large isometric hero scene showing {subject}. One clear
  focal object centered with generous white space around it.

CONCEPT ICON ROW (3-4 items)
  A horizontal row of 3-4 matching isometric concept icons illustrating
  {subject}. Identical style, scale and lighting across all icons,
  evenly spaced on white, each suitable for a bold label beneath it.

SINGLE ICON
  A single isometric concept icon representing {subject}, centered,
  with a soft long shadow, isolated on pure white.

STAT CALLOUT
  A bold infographic stat callout about {subject}: an oversized number
  as the focal point with a small isometric supporting illustration
  beside it.

COMPARISON (A vs B)
  A side-by-side isometric comparison illustrating {subject}: two
  balanced halves, clean dividing space, matched perspective and scale.

SYSTEM SCENE
  A wide isometric system scene showing {subject}: interconnected
  objects, nodes or paths, small figures, lots of airy white space.

SECTION HEADER BANNER
  A clean banner-style header graphic for the topic "{subject}": a blue
  map-pin or number badge and a thin azure underline rule, minimal,
  lots of white space, room for an uppercase title.

 ACCENT CLAUSE  (add one)
------------------------------------------------------------------
POSITIVE  : Use the signature eco gradient (mint #8FF3CC to emerald
            #38E6A6 to azure) as the accent on the positive/highlighted
            element.
NEGATIVE  : Use coral-pink #E27199 sparingly to mark the negative /
            warning element.
BOTH      : Contrast a positive element (eco gradient mint #8FF3CC to
            emerald #38E6A6) against a negative element (coral #E27199).
NEUTRAL   : Keep the palette to blues only — no green or coral accents.

------------------------------------------------------------------
 TEXT-IN-IMAGE CLAUSE  (add one)
------------------------------------------------------------------
NONE  : Do not include any text or numbers in the image.
LABEL : Include a short UPPERCASE label in a clean geometric sans-serif
        (Poppins/Montserrat style), deep blue #215DC3.
STAT  : Include one large bold stat number (eco-green #38E6A6 if
        positive, deep blue if neutral) with a small light-weight
        uppercase caption beneath it.
