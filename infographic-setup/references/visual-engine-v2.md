# Visual Engine v2.2 — Direction First, Renderer Appropriate

**Date:** 2026-08-02
**Status:** Active visual-production workflow
**Upstream:** `fast-post-loop-v1.md` for standard posts; `creative-genome-recombination-engine-v1.md`
and `creative-engine-v4-recombination.md` for declared flagships

## Thesis

The renderer is not the creative strategy. Select the reader promise, route-appropriate creative
input, and visual argument first. Then choose the surface that expresses it most clearly and
accurately.

| Layer | Job |
|---|---|
| Standard reference shelf or flagship Genome bundle | Attention, comprehension, utility, and bridge mechanics |
| Recombination brief | Flagship-only creative seed, ten concepts, three visible directions, and the selected assembly |
| Signature System V2 | Product-grade clarity plus a specialist Field Guide grammar for real working references; select the family from the reader task |
| Claim contract | Exact limits on numbers, outcomes, comparisons, and personal authority |
| Renderer | Execute the selected direction |
| Review | Decide whether the public asset is clear, useful, original, and true |

## Renderer selection

| Surface | Use when |
|---|---|
| Image engine | Selected illustration, editorial object, metaphor, scene, or unusual composition |
| HTML/SVG | A separate precise artifact where exact wording, data, geometry, interface, comparison, or repeatable control matters most |
| Spreadsheet | The working model, formulas, inputs, or decision table is the value |
| Document/PDF | A reference, field guide, checklist, or printable system is the value |
| Website UI | Interaction, user inputs, filtering, or repeated use materially improves the outcome |
| Motion | Sequence, change, comparison, or signal flow cannot be communicated as well in one still |

No surface is automatically more premium. Do not force illustration around a spreadsheet problem or
force a dashboard around a conceptual visual.

Do not use HTML or SVG as a title-and-card layer pasted over an image-engine scene. That hybrid is
neither a coherent rendered visual nor a useful exact artifact.

## Brand authority

Use `references/creative-review/signature-system-v2.md` as the active production guardrail. The
creative route must explicitly choose its visual family before a renderer is selected. When a Field
Guide is selected, follow `references/v5-field-guide-notebook-production-system.md`: it is a
specialist resource family, not an automatic template. The exact Shetty's Desk logo remains an owned
asset, never model-redrawn.

Operating Studio and `references/brand-kits/cobalt-grid/FRAME.md` are historical reference
material. Neither may be selected by default.

Creative references contribute structure, hierarchy, density, metaphor, and illustration method.
They do not replace the brand.

Use icon libraries only when the icon names a real tool, state, or action. Use official AI/tool marks
only for exact referential chips. Never construct the Shetty's Desk mark from third-party geometry.
Borrow an illustration repository's method - one cognitive action, a physical metaphor, whitespace,
and a clear shot - without copying its recurring character or distinctive style.

## Required visual inputs

- **Standard:** post-card.md with the selected route, two to four saved references, one visual move,
  short on-image wording, claim boundary, and active candidate paths.
- **Flagship:** reference bundle, recombination brief, content brief, and creative brief with the
  selected assembly and claim ledger.
- Selected visual family, signature guardrail, and exact owned or official assets when they
  materially matter.

Existing legacy packages retain their historical inputs for reproduction. New flagships use the
reference bundle; standard posts use the selected post card and its two-to-four-reference shelf.

## Visual contract

Before production:

1. Standard: use the Fast Post Loop and choose or kill three rough routes at minute 25. Flagship:
   develop three rough 4:5 directions with different story mechanisms.
2. Select, hybridise, or kill; do not use a final render as the first meaningful comparison.

Then write these sentences:

```md
At feed size, the reader sees:
The image makes this claim legible by showing:
The useful object or action is:
The eye moves from -> through -> to:
The caption adds, rather than repeats:
```

If those lines are vague, return to recombination. Do not repair a weak idea with style adjectives.

## Composition rules

- Use one deliberate reader response and one primary reading route. A framework, map, table, or
  sequence may use a clear progression instead of a single hero object.
- Embed supporting detail into the hero where possible instead of creating a wall of cards.
- Let whitespace clarify hierarchy; do not leave large areas empty without intent.
- Keep only text needed for recognition, use, or correct interpretation.
- Use exact mathematical notation for formula-led work.
- Keep source notes and qualifications legible but subordinate.
- Treat 3D/isometric scenes, flat editorial illustration, diagrams, charts, photographed artifacts,
  typographic references, and interfaces as equal families.
- Keep a visual understandable without opening the caption.

## Image-engine path

### Standard native scene

Compile the prompt from the selected `post-card.md`. Lock the reader tension, visible proof, useful
keep, short on-image wording, and claim boundary, but allow composition freedom.

```md
TASK:
Create one original, image-engine-native LinkedIn scene for Shetty's Desk.

READER AND LIVE MOMENT:
[reader, work moment, and why it matters]

VISIBLE TENSION:
[the collision or question the reader sees immediately]

PHYSICAL OR VISUAL MECHANISM:
[one object, route, boundary, transformation, or decision system]

USEFUL KEEP:
[one decision rule, distinction, or mental model embedded in the scene]

TEXT AND CLAIM BOUNDARY:
[only short integral wording; exact claims and prohibited inference]

COMPOSITION:
[one eye path; semantic green/coral if relevant; render as one native image]

AVOID:
HTML/SVG overlays, dashboard cards, title/footer bars, watermarks, model-redrawn logos, and
invented numbers, outcomes, or company proof.
```

### Flagship image-engine prompt

Use a prompt compiled from the selected recombination. Lock the text and meaning, but allow composition
freedom.

```md
TASK:
Create an original editorial LinkedIn visual for Shetty's Desk.

READER AND PAYOFF:
[reader, work moment, and useful outcome]

CREATIVE ASSEMBLY:
- Attention atom: [mechanism and transformation]
- Comprehension atom: [mechanism and transformation]
- Utility atom: [mechanism and transformation]
- Bridge atom: [mechanism and transformation]
- Anti-copy boundary: [what cannot be reproduced]

VISUAL ARGUMENT:
[reader response, eye path, and payoff]

CONTENT:
[exact title, labels, data, and placement homes]

CLAIM INTEGRITY:
[claim classes, sources, assumptions, and prohibited inference]

BRAND AND ASSETS:
[Selected visual family, signature guardrail, plus exact owned/official assets when they matter]

COMPOSITION FREEDOM:
[where the renderer may invent arrangement without inventing content]
```

Use positive instructions. Long negative prompt blocks usually make the image colder and more generic.

## Exact-control path

Use HTML/SVG, spreadsheet, document, Figma, or website UI when the value depends on exact content.
Publish it as its own usable artifact, not a finishing layer over an image-engine visual. A dense
Field Guide should have a Figma master with exact type, source/exception treatment, and an explicit
`field-guide-spec.json`; an image model may explore material but cannot typeset the final reference.
The control is not required for every image-model concept. Build it when it helps answer:

- Can the reader use the artifact without interpretation errors?
- Do the numbers, labels, and relationships fit honestly?
- Does interaction add value beyond a still?
- Would deterministic production better preserve the selected creative assembly?

## Complementary caption

The visual should provide the immediate model, framework, reference, or artifact. The caption should
add the operating context, reasoning, application, limitation, or Tiger judgment. Remove sentences
that merely read the image aloud.

## Review gate

For a standard post, use `five-reader-review.md` against the active visual. For a flagship or
precision artifact, use `templates/visual-output-review-template.md`. Publish only when:

- the problem or promise is visible in 3 seconds;
- the main insight is understood in 10 seconds;
- useful action is available in 30 seconds;
- the selected atoms are visible but the output does not resemble one source end to end;
- the image has one message and one intentional reading route;
- the result follows the selected visual family and signature guardrail;
- text, logos, formulas, relationships, and numbers are exact;
- each claim meets its support burden;
- the LinkedIn crop preserves comprehension and utility.

Hard fails:

- copied composition, artwork, distinctive wording, character, or branding;
- invented number, outcome, company proof, or personal authority;
- generic four-card dashboard or generic AI showroom;
- several competing heroes or reading routes;
- a beautiful image with no useful takeaway;
- visual and caption duplicating each other;
- third-party or model-redrawn Shetty's Desk logo.

## Motion

After the active visual named by `post-card.md` is approved, use `motion-engine-v1.md` only when
this sentence is strong:

> Motion helps because the reader needs to see [sequence or change] in [reading order].

Otherwise record a still-only decision. When motion is used, preserve exact text, assets, and opening
and closing frames; export both GIF and MP4 and run the motion audit.
