# Shetty's Desk — Story Archetypes

**Status:** active creative prompt card
**Purpose:** turn the saved-post north star into an original story choice before production.

This is not a content formula or a quality score. Pick one archetype when it gives the reader a
clearer reason to stop. Hybridise only when the visual still has one visible argument.

## The reusable engines

| Archetype | Reader opening | Visible proof mechanism | Primary job | Useful keep |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Identity gap → visible routes | “Where am I actually going?” | A map, ladder, or route system that lets the reader locate themselves | Qualified reach | A self-placement and next move |
| Famous move → operating-model reveal | “Why is this company doing the opposite of what I expected?” | A before/after operating model, not a news summary | Qualified reach | A lens for interpreting similar moves |
| Half-truth → missing dimension → decision rule | “This familiar answer is only half right.” | A comparison, gate, corridor, or decision boundary that makes the missing dimension visible | Saves and relevant follows | A rule for what to check or decide next |
| Misdiagnosis → complete working reference | “The problem is being calculated or managed the wrong way.” | A worked formula, timeline, diagnostic, or interpretation band | Saves and artifact use | A screenshot-ready reference |
| Broken foundation → maturity ladder | “The apparent capability problem starts one layer lower.” | A stacked foundation, weak base, or build order | Self-diagnosis and discussion | The next rung to strengthen |
| Role identity mirror → memorable metaphor | “That is exactly our team.” | A careful metaphor that maps recognisable roles to contributions and failure modes | Reach and sharing | A common language for the team |
| Timely signal → transparent index → human remainder | “What changes for my role because of this live event?” | A sourced index, exposure map, or ranking with an explicit human decision boundary | Timely reach and trust | A role-specific action list |

## What the local evidence says

- The career-map post proved the identity route engine can create discovery: 176,598 impressions,
  411 saves, and 338 followers.
- The Maersk operating-model case proved a named-company inversion can earn broad attention:
  57,087 impressions, 62 saves, and 115 followers.
- The planning-boundary and ABC/XYZ posts proved that a missing dimension plus decision rule can
  create high save/follow efficiency: 28,108 impressions / 65 saves / 42 followers and 8,962

## The one-page story card

Before a direction receives a polished visual, write only this:

```text
Archetype selected:
Primary post job: qualified reach / save / relevant follow / artifact use
Opening tension:
Visible proof of the tension:
Reader path: hook -> friction -> reveal -> action
Saveable keep:
What the caption adds that the image cannot:
```

If the visible proof is not apparent with the headline covered, redraw the concept. The image must
make the opening tension observable; the caption must advance the reader beyond it.
