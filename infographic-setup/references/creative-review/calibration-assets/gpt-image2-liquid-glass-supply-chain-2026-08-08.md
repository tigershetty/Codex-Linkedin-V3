## What the next creative system should learn

1. A single, designed **hero object** can carry the argument while smaller studies answer the
   supporting questions.
2. Material, depth, guide lines and inset views can make an educational graphic feel authored and
   worth opening; they cannot substitute for a real practitioner operation.
3. The strongest prompt ingredient is the *composition contract* — central object, supporting
   studies, annotations tied to visible details — not the named style or brand reference.
4. Use an image engine for scene-led/editorial material and illustration. Keep dense operational
   wording, tables and exact labels in Figma or another deterministic layout surface.

## Anti-copy boundary

Do not reproduce Apple's trade dress, colour system, distinctive glass treatment, copy, or this
reference's exact composition. Translate only the reader experience: a coherent central object,
supporting studies, visible relationships and deliberate annotation.
