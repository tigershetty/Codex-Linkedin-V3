# Shetty's Desk Signature System — Provisional V4

**Status:** active selection guardrail; the visual family is deliberately unresolved until the 480-post review is complete.

## What is fixed

Shetty's Desk should feel product-grade before it feels branded: one clear reader response, exceptional hierarchy, deliberate materials, no decorative clutter, and a visual decision that belongs to the story.

Every visual must explicitly select its family in the creative route. Valid families include editorial typography, framework/map, annotated real material, data reference, illustrated metaphor, spatial/3D scene, photographic artifact, and motion-led sequence. No family is a default.

## What is prohibited

- no default 2.5D or isometric operating scene;
- no default Cobalt Grid treatment;
- no permanent headline zone, footer, logo lock-up, lower takeaway strip, palette or hero object;
- no visual style selected before the reader tension and mechanism are chosen;
- no imitation of a saved creator's distinctive composition, art, wording or identity.

## What the completed review must decide

After all 480 records are manually reviewed or explicitly inaccessible, synthesize a small, explicit design system:

1. recurring composition principles that improve comprehension;
2. recurring materials and typography behaviours that make the work recognisable;
3. visual families to use, avoid, or reserve for specific reader jobs;
4. accessibility and feed-size rules;
5. a short anti-template list based on observed failure patterns.

Until then, the publication is recognisable through clarity, humane working moments, and editorial restraint—not a premature style mandate.
