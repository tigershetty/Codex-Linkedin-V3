## What is durable and should remain available

- **Top-100 retrieval corpus:** 60 direct, evidence-bound manual visual reviews are eligible for
  creative retrieval. Thirty-seven template-assisted records remain explicitly provisional and
  excluded from high-confidence retrieval. Separately, the 480-reference foundation ledger holds
  59 manually reviewed records and 421 explicitly inaccessible records. These are different
  collections/counts; neither means 480 visuals were manually reviewed.
- **Vincent Pierri corpus:** 53 source-hash-bound forensic records are preserved under
  `pierri-forensics/`. The transferable lesson is a *working information object*: a narrow work
  moment, an honest relational geometry, repeated micro-units, and a reliable scan path. It is
  not permission to imitate Pierri's marker handwriting, pastel skin, box geometry, moving-ball
  motif, copy, or layouts.
- **Source material policy:** raw Pierri media remains local at
  `infographic-setup/references/Vincent Pierri Reference/` and is deliberately ignored by Git.
  This repository carries its source-bound indexes, hashes and reviews, not redistributable raw
  media. Full raw-media validation requires that local collection beside the clone.

### 3. Practitioner language QA
