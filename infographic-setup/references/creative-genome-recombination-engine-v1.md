# Creative Genome and Recombination Engine v1

**Date:** 2026-08-02
**Status:** Active creative-intelligence authority for V4
**Applies before:** topic selection, research depth, captions, visuals, artifacts, and channel expansion

## Core decision

Tiger's saved posts are an intentionally curated positive creative dataset. A save already means
the post contained something worth retaining: a hook, visual, explanation, framework, utility,
story, product idea, or combination of these.

Do not ask whether a saved post is good enough to use. Ask:

> What made it worth saving, and how can that mechanism be transformed into original value for a
> planner, buyer, or supply-chain transformation leader?

Saved references validate creative possibility. Tiger's published results later show whether a
mechanism transferred to this account, audience, topic, and execution. A weak Tiger result never
removes the positive label from the source reference.

## The four intelligence layers

| Layer | Fixed job |
|---|---|
| Saved posts and Top-100 | Creative north star: attention, explanation, visual grammar, utility, and value bridge |
| Tiger performance | Transfer learning: what produced reach, saves, follows, clicks, and artifact use on this account |
| Audience, market, and timely signals | Where a live role, decision, pain, ambition, or current change deserves attention |
| Claim support and Tiger voice | What may be stated, how strongly, and what judgment makes it Shetty's Desk |

Never collapse these layers into one quality score. Relevance ranking is not proof, performance is
not taste validation, and a creative reference is not factual support for our claim.

## The creative genome

The genome is a searchable decomposition of the complete content-intelligence database. Every
saved record has `positive_creative_signal=true`. Its annotation status records only how deeply
the mechanics have been inspected.

Decompose each reference into:

- **attention:** opening move, tension, specificity, identity trigger, curiosity device;
- **argument:** sequence, reveal, compression, payoff timing, completeness;
- **visual:** dominant object, reading route, hierarchy, density, illustration role, proof display;
- **utility:** map, framework, checklist, formula, reference, workflow, template, tool, or decision rule;
- **tone:** analytical, instructional, contrarian, personal, urgent, reassuring, or aspirational;
- **bridge:** on-platform value, next action, destination value, reason to return;
- **creative atoms:** the smallest mechanisms that may be transformed without copying;
- **anti-copy boundary:** subject matter, wording, branding, artwork, and distinctive expression that must not be reproduced.

Preview presence never counts as a verified visual annotation. Inspect the actual image or a local
Top-100 asset before a visual atom is marked `visually_verified`.

## Creative retrieval

Begin every LinkedIn post, flagship Substack issue, and website artifact with a structured query:

1. primary reader and work moment;
2. decision, pain, ambition, or curiosity;
3. intended reader payoff;
4. possible content modes and artifacts;
5. patterns recently overused by Shetty's Desk;
6. optional freshness facets such as news, regulation, company moves, research, or tool changes.

Retrieve 10-15 saved references by **fit**, not by a universal quality score. The shortlist should
contain enough variety to escape the first obvious format.

## The reference bundle

Build one bundle with four creative jobs:

1. **Attention reference** - earns the stop through its opening tension or promise.
2. **Comprehension reference** - makes the idea legible through hierarchy and reading route.
3. **Utility reference** - creates a save-worthy object or usable takeaway.
4. **Bridge reference** - continues value naturally into a website artifact, Substack issue, or return loop.

One post may fill several jobs. Use two to four curated references, plus one or two separate Tiger
precedents when they improve the transfer hypothesis. Coherence comes from one reader promise and
explicit atom roles, not from artificially narrowing the creative source pool.

For every selected atom record:

- a stable `creative_element_id` beginning with `CE-`;
- exact source ID and URL or local asset;
- atom type: attention, argument, visual, utility, tone, or bridge;
- exact mechanism being borrowed;
- supply-chain transformation;
- what must not be copied;
- why it supports the same reader promise as the other atoms.

## Recombination, not imitation

Do not adapt one creator end to end. Generate at least ten concepts by recombining different atoms
across the shortlist. Change the argument, visual object, utility, or value bridge - not merely the
headline wording.

Develop at least three genuinely different concept families before selection. First turn each into
a rough visible 4:5 direction tile using `templates/creative-direction-sprint-template.md`. A
direction is not a different headline, colour treatment, or icon. It has a different reader moment,
first-frame hook, visual primitive, and useful keep. A useful default spread is:

- **Utility:** the clearest tool, map, reference, or working artifact;
- **Visual idea:** the strongest metaphor, transformation, contrast, or physical object;
- **Narrative:** the strongest reveal, case, identity shift, or before-and-after logic.

These are exploration prompts, not fixed quotas. A stronger fourth direction may replace any of
them. Run a forced select / hybridise / kill comparison before claim support, calculator work,
finished captions, or visual polishing. Select the direction with the clearest combined reader
payoff, not the safest production path.

## Reader contract

Backstage intelligence may be complex. The public output must be simple:

- the problem, tension, or promise is visible in **3 seconds**;
- the main insight is understandable in **10 seconds**;
- a useful action, reference, or next step is available in **30 seconds**;
- the visual has one dominant message and one primary reading route;
- the caption and visual add different value instead of duplicating each other.

## Content modes and claim support

Choose the content mode after creative exploration. Evidence burden follows each claim, not the
entire content piece.

| Content or claim mode | Minimum support |
|---|---|
| Framework, checklist, visual map, or workflow | Correct logic, attribution where applicable, scope, and failure boundary |
| Tiger interpretation or operating recommendation | Approved Tiger source; clearly presented as judgment or a proposed rule |
| Descriptive number | Exact source, date, unit, population or scope, and limitation |
| Calculated number or formula | Traceable inputs, sourced method, assumptions, and reproducible calculation |
| Named company success or failure | Documented case, who reported it, exact outcome, and no generalization beyond the case |
| Comparative or causal claim | Evidence capable of supporting the comparison; otherwise narrow the wording |
| Current tool or AI workflow | Current official capability source, real inputs and outputs or a documented deployment, validation, and human owner |
| Opinion or synthesis | Tiger reasoning plus sourcing for any factual substrate |
| Simulation or hypothesis | Explicitly selected and labelled; never presented as a real outcome or the default proof device |

When support is insufficient, narrow or remove the unsupported claim. Do not automatically discard
the topic. A useful framework does not need a fabricated numerical case to deserve publication.

Internal software fixtures may test logic. They must stay clearly test-only and cannot appear as a
public result, customer outcome, case study, or reason to care.

## Operating flow

```text
Measure Tiger transfer
  -> scan audience and current signals
  -> retrieve 10-15 saved references
  -> decompose the useful creative atoms
  -> recombine into a creative seed + 10 concepts
  -> make 3 distinct visible direction tiles
  -> force a select / hybridise / kill decision
  -> select content mode and reader promise
  -> apply claim-proportionate support
  -> add Tiger judgment where needed
  -> build visual, caption, and optional artifact
  -> publish and record atom-level transfer
```

Research may run during exploration when facts help reveal an idea. It becomes a formal gate only
for the claims whose burden requires it.

## Per-package files

Every new major content package uses:

- `reference-query.json` - the retrieval intent;
- `reference-candidates.json` - deterministic shortlist;
- `reference-bundle.json` - selected atoms and anti-copy boundaries;
- `recombination-brief.md` - ten concepts, three developed directions, and the selected assembly;
- `creative-seed.md` and `directions/` - the work moment and three rough direction tiles;
- `content-brief-v2.md` - reader, mode, claim contract, channel job, and artifact decision;
- production files appropriate to the selected direction.

## Commands

From `infographic-setup/`:

```bash
node scripts/build-creative-genome.mjs
node scripts/retrieve-creative-references.mjs \
  --query data/{week}/{slug}/reference-query.json \
  --output data/{week}/{slug}/reference-candidates.json
node scripts/compile-recombination-brief.mjs \
  --candidates data/{week}/{slug}/reference-candidates.json \
  --output data/{week}/{slug}/recombination-brief.md
node scripts/validate-reference-bundle.mjs \
  --input data/{week}/{slug}/reference-bundle.json
```

The active snapshot is declared in `references/creative-genome/active-manifest.json`. The source
audit remains immutable and versioned.

## Learning loop

At 24 hours, Day 7, and Day 28, connect results to the bundle and creative atoms used:

- attention: impressions, profile visits, and qualified reach;
- utility: saves, sends, downloads, starts, and completions;
- trust: relevant followers, replies, repeat readers, and return use;
- bridge: Substack visits, subscriptions, website visits, and artifact use.

Record an overall `transfer_result` and an `element_transfer` result for every accepted
`creative_element_id`: `transferred`, `unclear`, or `did_not_transfer`. Never rewrite the source
reference's positive creative signal.

## Future levers

News, trends, social listening, Substack momentum, regulation, company moves, and tool releases are
query facets, not new operating systems. Add them to the signal layer and freshness fields without
changing the genome, recombination, claim-support, or learning contracts.
