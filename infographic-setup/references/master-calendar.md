## Weekly View — Posts 1–4 Structure

Each week publishes 4 posts across both series:

| Post | Series | Type |
|---|---|---|
| Post 1 | SC 101 Advanced | Foundational concept — accessible to any audience |
| Post 2 | SC 101 Advanced | Foundational concept — builds on Post 1 |
| Post 3 | AI for Supply Chain | Practical AI workflow for a specific SC role (Post A) |
| Post 4 | AI for Supply Chain | Practical AI workflow for a different SC role (Post B) |

Posts 1–2 teach the WHAT. Posts 3–4 show the HOW using AI.

## How to Use

**To run SC 101:**
```
/101 [topic-number]
```
Topics in: `references/101-plan.md`
Tracker: `data/101-series-tracker.md`

**To run AI for Supply Chain:**
```
/ai-for-sc W[##]              → both posts for that week
/ai-for-sc W[##] [slug]       → one specific post
```
Topics in: `references/ai-for-sc-plan-v2.md`
Tracker: `data/ai-for-sc-series-tracker.md`
