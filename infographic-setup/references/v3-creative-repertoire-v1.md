# V3 Creative Repertoire v1

**Purpose:** a small shelf of visual moves we can recombine quickly. This is a repertoire, not a
new scorecard.

V3 is a rendering language: tangible operating objects, luminous white and blue depth, semantic
green and coral, and spatial logic that works before the caption. Its signature is an authored
technical cover plus a working instrument—not one repeated board layout or generic 3D logistics
scene.

## 1. Pressure gate

- **Reader moment:** release, wait, freeze, escalate, or resequence before a real boundary closes.
- **Visual move:** one literal gate, fence, cut-off, runway, or commitment line with outcomes on
  either side.
- **Keep:** the decision rule that decides whether the reader can cross it.
- **Fast test:** headline, three named objects, one boundary, one outcome. Stop there.
- **Use when:** a missing limit or decision boundary is the real story.
- **Source shelf:** data/2026-W31/mps-as-a-production-commitment/visual.png,
  data/2026-W32/ship-now-vs-consolidate/visual-v3.png, references/top 100/33.jpeg.

## 2. Operating-model flip

- **Reader moment:** a familiar company or team makes an apparently contradictory move.
- **Visual move:** the same actor inside two different flows; show what changed in ownership,
  constraint, or route.
- **Keep:** a lens for recognising the same change elsewhere.
- **Fast test:** one question, one before flow, one after flow, three changed nodes.
- **Use when:** a named-company move or industry change deserves broad reach.
- **Source shelf:** references/top 100/65.jpeg and the Maersk transfer note in the content-market
  audit.

## 3. Pocket field card

- **Reader moment:** what should I calculate, what does it mean, and what do I do next?
- **Visual move:** one large formula, test, or reference object with three inputs and one
  interpretation.
- **Keep:** a screenshot-ready working reference.
- **Fast test:** real formula logic, a tiny variable legend, and one interpretation strip.
- **Use when:** precision itself is the reader value.
- **Source shelf:** references/top 100/5.jpeg and
  data/2026-W30/optimal-batch-size-decision-board/visual.png.

## 4. Role-collision tableau

- **Reader moment:** functions pull the same order, buffer, supplier decision, or capacity slot
  in different directions.
- **Visual move:** one shared operating object in the middle and distinct role pressures around it.
- **Keep:** language for a conflict a team already feels.
- **Fast test:** four roles or hands, one exact pressure per role, one shared consequence.
- **Use when:** recognition and shareability matter more than a technical walkthrough.
- **Source shelf:** references/top 100/64.jpeg and the six-path career-map transfer note.

## 5. You-are-here terrain

- **Reader moment:** where are we now, and what is the next move?
- **Visual move:** a route, terrain, ladder, or maturity landscape with a current marker and one
  next step.
- **Keep:** self-placement and an actionable next move.
- **Fast test:** four levels or routes, one marker, and one destination condition.
- **Use when:** career, capability, transformation maturity, or AI adoption is the real reader job.
- **Source shelf:** references/top 100/71.jpeg and references/top 100/87.jpeg.

## 6. Operating trace

- **Reader moment:** an uncertain time, readiness window, queue, demand signal, or capacity loss
  changes a real decision before anyone has time to explain it.
- **Visual move:** turn that invisible variable into a material trace that bends, crosses, blocks,
  fills, spills, layers, or resolves inside one operating world.
- **Keep:** a memorable way to see what actually makes the option unsafe, not another generic rule.
- **Fast test:** remove the trace. If the image still says the same thing, it was decorative and the
  route has not earned production.
- **Use when:** the reader needs to see change, uncertainty, or pressure act on a physical system.
- **Source shelf:** references/top 100/65.jpeg, references/top 100/44.jpeg, and
  data/2026-W31/mps-as-a-production-commitment/visual.png.

## 7. Forensic operating atlas

- **Reader moment:** a familiar object, formula, public case, or physical product hides the real
  decision system.
- **Visual move:** turn that evidence object into a museum-grade technical plate, exploded view,
  cutaway, or working map where its structure reveals the operating consequence.
- **Keep:** one source-backed test, comparison, or model the reader can use beyond the example.
- **Fast test:** cover the labels. If the reader cannot still name the object and proposition, the
  image is too generic; kill it rather than adding a dashboard.
- **Use when:** public data, formulas, process mechanics, named-company operating cases, or
  verifiable AI workflows provide the actual reason to care.
- **Source shelf:** references/top 100/5.jpeg, references/top 100/44.jpeg, and
  data/2026-W31/bom-to-mps-connection/visual.png.

## Anti-pattern

Do not turn every route into a dashboard. The point is to make the reader feel a real operating
moment first, then give them one useful mental object to take away.
