# Creative Retrieval Guide

**Status:** Active companion to the Top-100 Creative Genome  
**Purpose:** choose a packaging mechanism *after* a real opportunity is selected, without letting the reference library narrow the subject matter.

## The correct order

```text
real reader moment / tension
  → intended reader response
  → claim mode and support boundary
  → retrieve creative mechanisms
  → form three visibly different routes
  → select one original assembly
```

The genome does not supply the first line. It tells us how to make a selected line easier to stop for, understand, save, share or discuss.

## Five-question retrieval card

Before opening the genome, write only these five answers:

1. What is the reader experiencing right now: confusion, friction, hidden waste, choice overload, ambition, uncertainty, recognition, or curiosity?
2. What response should the post earn: recognition, understanding, practical use, or memorable intrigue?
3. What cognitive job is required: distinguish, orient, sequence, classify, assess progress, make a change visible, or make an object tangible?
4. What claim mode is planned: framework, sourced fact, calculation, case, or judgment?
5. What must the visual prove before the caption is opened?

This preserves creativity: none of these questions select the subject, visual style or final headline.

## Retrieval rules

- Pull two to four *different* families, not four executions from one creator.
- One route can lead with attention, another with comprehension, another with utility. They should differ in visual primitive and reader action, not just copy.
- A framework may be useful without outcome data; a numerical, causal, company or personal claim still needs traceable support.
- Use the record-level `use_when` and `do_not_use_when` conditions as guards, not as topic suggestions.
- Treat every source’s exact words, subject matter, artwork, visual identity and distinctive composition as non-transferable.
- If the mechanism does not make the opening tension visible in three seconds, choose another one.

## Quick mechanism selector

| If the selected opportunity needs… | First family to inspect | Second family for contrast |
|---|---|---|
| a clean boundary between concepts | comparison | taxonomy or system map |
| orientation across relationships | system map | data landscape |
| a repeatable start-to-finish path | workflow | field guide |
| a compact reference worth returning to | field guide | taxonomy |
| a credible sense of progression | maturity | before/after |
| a difficult pattern made recognisable | metaphor | before/after |
| a visible intervention or change | before/after | system map |
| a real tool, guide or working artifact | product proof | collection |
| a bounded market or option overview | data landscape | taxonomy |
| a real body of useful work | collection | product proof |

This table is a mechanism lookup—not a content calendar and not an evidence shortcut.

## What gets recorded after selection

Each new post card records the reader moment, selected response, three routes considered, exact Top-100 record IDs, transferable atoms, anti-copy boundary, claim support, Tiger input (if any), and the chosen visual/caption linkage. Its results later become transfer learning for this account.
