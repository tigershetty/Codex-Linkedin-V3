# Content Demand Proof Gate v1

**Date:** 2026-07-20  
**Status:** Mandatory before research lock, premium rendering, motion, or resource construction  
**Applies to:** Supply Chain 101 and AI for Supply Chain

## Purpose

The calendar proposes topics. It does not authorize production.

This gate prevents visual potential, resource potential, or calendar sequence from compensating for weak audience demand. A post may be useful without becoming a premium artifact. Production depth follows evidence.

## Gate A: Audience evidence

Record at least three dated receipts from at least two source types.

Accepted source types:

- a comment or direct message from the intended audience;
- a repeated workplace question, meeting failure, or practitioner interview;
- search demand, community discussion, event question, or current industry change;
- a previous Shetty's Desk post with relevant saves, comments, follows, or reach;
- a credible creator or research source with visible audience response;
- a product/tool change verified in official documentation.

Each receipt must include the audience role, exact language or faithful summary, URL or local evidence path, date observed, and what it implies. A generic trend article is not an audience receipt by itself.

```md
Receipt ID:
Source type:
Audience role:
Observed language or behavior:
URL / local evidence:
Observed date:
Topic implication:
```

**Pass:** three receipts, two source types, one from the target role or a credible proxy.  
**Fail:** evidence is invented, undated, aesthetic-only, or only says the topic is important.

## Gate B: Shetty's Desk performance prior

Find the closest three historical posts by audience job, not only subject.

```md
Historical post:
Audience job: locate / diagnose / decide / compare / execute / debate
Impressions:
Interactions:
Saves:
Followers gained:
What likely transferred:
What failed:
Confidence in inference:
```

**Pass:** the brief names what it will repeat and what it will change.  
**Fail:** the prior is omitted because a Top-100 reference looks strong.

## Gate C: Tiger authority

At least one must be true:

- Tiger witnessed or performed the work;
- Tiger can describe a repeated practitioner pattern and its boundary;
- Tiger ran a documented experiment or analyzed original data;
- Tiger can synthesize credible research into a genuinely new supply-chain decision frame;
- a named practitioner will review or contribute before publication.

The brief must name the source of the authority. "Tiger has experience in supply chain" is not enough.

## Gate D: Argument

Complete this before choosing the final carrier:

```md
Recognizable tension:
Tiger's belief:
Evidence:
Counterargument or boundary:
Decision or behavior that changes:
Why this matters now:
```

**Pass:** a specific professional should recognize, reconsider, or decide something differently.  
**Fail:** the outcome is only "the reader understands the concept."

## Gate E: Reference provenance

For every lead reference, record the fields defined in `top100-creator-attribution-and-audit-2026-07.md`.

- `reference-informed` may guide visual or writing mechanics.
- `performance-supported` requires a live source and observed response or business evidence.
- An unsigned, reposted, missing, or low-confidence reference cannot be the sole lead reference.

## Gate F: Pre-render challenge

Before a premium still, motion build, or full resource:

1. draft the selected hook;
2. draft a 150-word argument;
3. sketch the minimum explanatory structure;
4. ask 3-5 relevant people or independent reviewers what is wrong, missing, obvious, or worth saving;
5. record what changed.

For lower-risk evergreen posts, one domain reviewer plus two evidence-based internal challenges may substitute. The brief must state why.

## Production-depth decision

| Evidence state | Allowed carrier | Resource rule |
|---|---|---|
| Strong demand + strong authority + strong argument | Premium still, motion, or data artifact | Score after post package is approved; build when demand or strategic value warrants it. |
| Strong tension but untested frame | Text, photo, poll with analysis, rough diagram, or compact table | Do not build a full resource. Test first. |
| Strong technical value but narrow audience | Medium-depth reference card or article | Lightweight guide only if directly useful. |
| Weak or inferred demand | Park or reframe | No resource. |

## Final gate record

```md
Gate A audience evidence: pass / fail
Gate B historical prior: pass / fail
Gate C Tiger authority: pass / fail
Gate D argument: pass / fail
Gate E reference provenance: pass / fail
Gate F pre-render challenge: pass / fail / not-yet-required

Production depth: flagship / medium / test / park
Carrier:
Decision owner:
Decision date:
```

No numeric score can override a failed Gate A, C, D, or E. Gate F must pass before flagship rendering.
