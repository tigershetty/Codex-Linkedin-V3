# Creative Opportunity Selection

**Date:** 2026-08-02
**Status:** Active replacement for the numeric V4 topic scorecard

## Purpose

Select work with creative range and audience value without turning every idea into an evidence-heavy
artifact or forcing a fragile 100-point score.

## Four valid starting paths

- **Problem-led:** a planner, buyer, or transformation leader has a recurring decision or friction.
- **Pattern-led:** a saved post reveals a powerful format, visual object, simplification, or utility that
  can be transformed for supply chain.
- **Evidence-led:** a public dataset, method, case, formula, or research finding contains a useful story.
- **Timely-led:** a current event, regulation, company move, or tool change alters a real professional decision.

No path outranks the others. Each must converge on a clear reader payoff and a route-appropriate
creative input: a two-to-four-reference shelf and post card for a standard post, or a full Creative
Genome bundle for a declared flagship.

## Selection board

Use these fields instead of a numeric score:

| Field | Decision question |
|---|---|
| Reader | Who should stop, and in what work moment? |
| Care statement | Why should this person care now or repeatedly? |
| Creative opportunity | Which saved mechanics make the subject more interesting, legible, useful, or shareable? |
| Range | Standard: can three rough routes produce a non-generic visible proof? Flagship: can ten concepts and three meaningfully different directions do so? |
| Content mode | Framework, reference, workflow, tool, case, analysis, opinion, narrative, or hybrid? |
| Useful payoff | What can the reader understand, decide, adapt, save, use, or question? |
| Claim contract | Which claims are planned and what support does each require? |
| Tiger contribution | Is fresh Tiger judgment needed, available, or intentionally absent? |
| Channel job | Does LinkedIn stand alone, and what additional value earns Substack or website continuation? |
| Portfolio role | Does this add a new audience, problem, creative family, utility, or timely angle? |

## Decisions

- **Explore:** the reader or angle is promising; choose its route, retrieve the proportionate
  references, and make rough routes before judging it.
- **Build:** one direction has a clear reader contract, coherent standard shelf or flagship bundle,
  appropriate claim support, and channel job.
- **Park:** no clear reader payoff, no useful creative transformation, or the central claim cannot be supported or narrowed.

Do not park a framework merely because it lacks company outcome data. Do not build a numerical or
causal claim merely because the visual is attractive.

## Rejection conditions

Park or reframe only when one of these remains true after exploration:

- the reader and work moment are still vague;
- the public output has no understandable payoff;
- the selected route collapses into the same generic format (three rough standard routes or ten
  flagship concepts, as applicable);
- the chosen reference atoms do not support one coherent promise;
- the central factual, numerical, causal, company, or personal claim exceeds available support and
  cannot be narrowed;
- the deeper destination merely repeats the LinkedIn post;
- the output depends on invented authority, experience, result, or customer proof.
