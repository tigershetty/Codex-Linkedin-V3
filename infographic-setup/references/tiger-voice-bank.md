## Purpose

This bank stores approved evidence that can make future writing more recognisably Tiger without teaching the system from unapproved AI drafts. It is deliberately selective.

Add only:

- beliefs or positions Tiger explicitly approves for reuse;
- corrections that reveal a durable voice preference;
- final published samples Tiger says sound right;
- credentials and authority claims explicitly approved for public use;
- restrictions that prevent unsafe or inaccurate personalisation.

Do not add raw private transcripts, confidential workplace details, speculative inferences, untouched AI copy, or every minor line edit.

## Promotion Rule

`tiger-voice.md` changes only after Tiger explicitly approves the change. Repeated evidence across three approved samples is the preferred threshold for promoting a correction or pattern from this bank into the definitive Voice DNA.
