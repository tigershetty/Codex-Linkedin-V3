# Top-100 Creator Attribution and System Audit

**Date:** 2026-07-20  
**Status:** Active research record  
**Purpose:** turn the Top-100 from an aesthetic inspiration folder into an auditable source of creator, artifact, caption, performance, and funnel intelligence.

## Executive finding

The Top-100 is not a set of 100 independently proven creator systems.

- It contains 97 local visual files; references 6, 12, and 75 have captions but no local image.
- A small number of creators dominate the set: Asmaa Gad (about 14), Eric Partaker (about 9, including one duplicate), Tom Mills (4), Charlie Hills (4), Yonathan Levy (4), Premium Ghostwriting Academy (4), and several three-post clusters.
- Some rows are reposts or third-party captions attached to another person's artifact.
- Some visually polished references have little public engagement. Inclusion proves that somebody selected the artifact, not that the audience wanted it.
- The corpus is strongest as a visual-mechanics library. It must not be used as performance proof unless a live post, creator system, and audience response are also recorded.

## Attribution rules

1. `Artifact creator` means the person or organization whose name, brand, or original credit appears on the visual or in a credible original post.
2. `Poster` is recorded separately when the workbook caption is a repost, commentary, or share.
3. `High` confidence means the image, caption, and a live primary surface agree.
4. `Medium` confidence means two sources agree but the original post or local image is missing.
5. `Low` means the visual is unsigned and no indexed original was found. Do not adapt creator-specific style from a low-confidence row.

## Counterexamples and cautions

- [Alper Ozel's maturity-grid post](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/alper-ozel-723ab511_supply-chain-grid-assess-maturity-unlock-activity-7433021723471601664-quyq) is comprehensive and visually polished but showed only a small number of reactions in indexed results. Density is not demand.
- Generic process explainers such as an unsigned KPI dashboard or a standard S&OP map may be accurate but offer no creator-specific claim, proof, or reason to discuss them.
- A branded repost can transfer visual credibility while obscuring the original creator. This prevents reliable learning unless original and poster are separated.
- Follower counts, comment counts, and site-reported impressions are directional evidence, not controlled comparisons. Record the observation date and never imply causation from one post.
