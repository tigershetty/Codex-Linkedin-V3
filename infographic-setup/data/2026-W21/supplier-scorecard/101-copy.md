## LinkedIn Caption

Why does everyone know which supplier is underperforming but nobody can prove it?

Because gut feel is not a scorecard, and without one, the conversation stays stuck at opinion.

Supplier scorecards are not about rating suppliers, they are about what you weight. Score delivery at 30% and cost at 10%, and you have just told your supplier team what this company actually values. Most scorecards send the wrong message by accident.

This is Episode 25 of Supply Chain 101. From here, we go one function deep per month.

We start with procurement, because it sits at the beginning of every supply chain and shapes everything that comes after it.

A supplier scorecard gives you the one thing most procurement conversations are missing: a number that everyone agreed on in advance. Five criteria cover what actually matters:

• Quality is defect rate and spec compliance. It determines whether the product works. Most teams track this, but track it inconsistently because the threshold was never written down.

• Delivery is on-time-in-full against the date you needed it, not the date the supplier promised. Those two dates are often weeks apart, and measuring the wrong one masks the real performance.

• Cost is not unit price alone. It includes price stability, expediting charges, and rework costs that spread across four departments and never appear on the same report.

• Responsiveness is how fast they engage when something goes wrong. It is the most revealing criterion and the most skipped, because nobody thinks to score it until a crisis makes it obvious.

• Compliance is certifications, documentation, audit results. Low weight until it becomes the only weight.

The weighting row is the most important row in the scorecard. A supplier scoring 74% overall can look acceptable until you unpack it and find their delivery is flagged red. The total hides what the breakdown exposes. What you weight is what you value, and your suppliers will read that row before they read their score.

What would your most important supplier score today?
