## 4. Research Thesis

**Non-obvious thesis:** demand planning fails less because people cannot predict demand and more because teams do not know which decision the forecast is supposed to inform.  
**Counterargument / caveat:** forecast accuracy still matters; the point is that accuracy is only useful when it changes inventory, capacity, sourcing, production, or customer actions.  
**What people get wrong:** they treat the forecast as the answer, when the forecast is only one input into a demand plan that requires assumptions, accountability, and trade-offs.  
**Meeting-room use:** use before a demand review or S&OP pre-meeting to ask: what changed, why did it change, and which decision now changes because of it?

## 5. Content Shape

**Hook direction:** Contrarian / Comparison-Gap  
**Opening line candidate:** A forecast is not the decision. It is the input to the decision.  
**Core explanation:** separate signal, decomposition, forecast, demand plan, and operating decision; show how each stage changes what the team should do next.  
**Reusable takeaway:** a demand planning decision book that maps each forecast conversation to the decision it should affect.  
**CTA question:** If your forecast missed by 20% tomorrow, which decision would your team change first?

## 7. AI-for-SC Only

**Role:** Demand Planner  
**Recurring frustration:** forecast misses create the same debate every month because no one decomposes the error or decides what changes operationally.  
**Input data required:** historical demand, forecast, actuals, promotional events, inventory position, capacity constraints, customer order signals, assumptions log  
**AI workflow:** decompose variance, identify pattern type, test forecast value-add, draft the demand review narrative, and recommend decision options for human review  
**Verification step:** compare AI explanation against source data and planner assumptions; never accept a causal explanation without operational evidence  
**When NOT to use AI:** final demand-plan ownership, customer allocation decisions, or capacity commitments without human sign-off  
**Reusable artifact:** decision book / meeting narrative / checklist
