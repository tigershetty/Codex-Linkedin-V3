## Creative Direction

Build a new artifact architecture:

> Demand Planning Decision Table with an Evidence Rail.

This should feel like a high-end demand review artifact on a luminous glass planning table, not like the Supply Chain Resilience OS with a new hero swapped in.

The hero is a large angled transparent acrylic decision table occupying the central field. It has one dominant forecast-miss path running across it, with labeled evidence feeding into the decision zone. The surrounding scene should be a quiet demand-review room: soft planning-wall glow, faint calendar grid, blurred data wall, and precise glass layers. Avoid the SC OS-style row of supply-chain miniatures around the top or bottom.
