## Brand System

- Same Shetty's Desk brand story as SC OS: luminous white and pale blue space, azure structure, navy ink, subtle eco-green for positive learning/progress, coral only for forecast miss / bias / exception.
- Premium physical staging: glassy acrylic overlays, vellum sheets, thin grid lines, blue technical marks, subtle shadows, high-status desk objects.
- Use depth and material carefully: layered paper, transparent overlays, small physical pins, refined route lines, fine chart traces.
- Avoid chunky app icons, toy-like objects, saturated cartoon buttons, beige scrapbook colors, and generic dashboard cards.

## Visual Mechanics

- SIGNALS = layered translucent tabs feeding into the board.
- VARIANCE = forecast-vs-actual curve with one coral miss band.
- ASSUMPTIONS = pinned assumption cards / scenario chips, no body text.
- DECISION = elegant branching decision paths to the four endpoints.
- Add a subtle LEARN loop as a green return line from decision back to signals.

The board should feel authored and useful, with fewer large modules but more refined micro-detail: tick marks, annotations without words, alignment rails, route dots, ledger lines, small dimensional objects, and precise spacing.
