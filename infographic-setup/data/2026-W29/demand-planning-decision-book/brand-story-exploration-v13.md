# Brand Story Exploration v13 — Two Demand Planning Candidates

**Date:** 2026-07-03  
**Status:** candidates / not final  

## Design Correction

The goal is not to copy Supply Chain Resilience OS. The goal is to keep the same Shetty's Desk brand story while choosing a topic-specific artifact architecture.

Shared brand story:

- two-tone SC OS-style title treatment,
- luminous white / pale-blue environment,
- navy and azure structural language,
- eco-green only for learning/progress,
- coral only for forecast miss / exception,
- premium planning desk staging,
- restrained Shetty's Desk footer authorship.

## Candidate A

**File:** `gpt-image-2-candidate-v13a-decision-table.png`  
**Artifact:** Demand Planning Decision Table  
**Architecture:** four-lane decision table: signals -> variance -> assumptions -> decision endpoints  

**What works:**

- Strong SC OS-style title treatment.
- Clear, executive-readable decision flow.
- Higher polish than the earlier warm workbook and v12 board.
- Practical demand review logic is easy to follow.

**Risk:**

- Still has a lane/table structure that may feel closer to a premium dashboard than a wholly new artifact.

## Candidate B

**File:** `gpt-image-2-candidate-v13b-assumption-map.png`  
**Artifact:** Demand Planning Assumption Map  
**Architecture:** central forecast miss path with assumption layer and decision gates around it  

**What works:**

- More differentiated from SC OS.
- Stronger topic fit: the forecast miss and assumptions become the hero.
- Maintains the brand story through color, title, desk staging, and footer authorship.
- Feels more like a new artifact from the same designer rather than a repeated layout.

**Risk:**

- Some icon treatments are still literal and could be made more premium in the next pass.

## Current Recommendation

Use Candidate B as the better strategic direction, then refine:

- reduce chunky icon language,
- increase artifact materiality,
- make the central assumption map feel more like an authored physical object,
- preserve the SC OS-style two-tone heading.
