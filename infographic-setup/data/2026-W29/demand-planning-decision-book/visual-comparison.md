## 7. v15 Learning

- Background realism matters as much as foreground polish. Synthetic corridor/glow treatment can make an otherwise strong image feel AI-generated.
- The winning background language is a human-edited demand-review studio: matte texture, faint printed planning boards, tactile tabletop shadows, and restrained depth.
- Correct words are not enough. Labels must point to the exact object they describe.
- Context-labeled icons make the artifact useful. Anonymous decorative icons reduce trust.
