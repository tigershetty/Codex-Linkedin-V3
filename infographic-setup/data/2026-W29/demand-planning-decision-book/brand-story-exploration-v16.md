## What Improved

### Table

- Background now feels closer to SC OS and v16B: minimal white / pale-blue, luminous, and less like a blue data-wall scene.
- The evidence rail and table logic remain intact.
- The icon depth and acrylic board elevation are preserved.
- The decision actions remain readable and operational.

### Lens

- Background returned to the minimal white / pale-blue Shetty's Desk system.
- The sci-fi corridor and glow were reduced without adding office clutter.
- The icon depth and glass/acrylic elevation are preserved better than v15.
- The semantic issue is materially improved by replacing ambiguous `FORECAST` / `ACTUAL` arrows with a legend inside the lens.
- `ROOT ASSUMPTION` now points to a central bullseye marker instead of being confused with the two demand curves.

## QA Read

- `FORECAST` = blue line sample in legend + blue curve.
- `ACTUAL` = coral line sample in legend + coral curve.
- `MISS` = translucent coral gap.
- `ROOT ASSUMPTION` = bullseye marker.
- Decision endpoints remain readable and dimensional.

## Remaining Risk

The generated Shetty's Desk logo is acceptable for concept review in both v16 candidates, but the final should still receive exact-logo production treatment if promoted to `visual.png`.

## Recommendation

v16B is currently the most distinctive Demand Planning visual direction because it restores the white brand field, keeps the elevated depth, and fixes the forecast/actual/root-assumption ambiguity without flattening the artifact.

v16A is the selected final because it is the safer, more immediately readable business artifact and fits the user's chosen table direction.
