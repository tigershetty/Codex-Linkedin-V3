# Brand Story Exploration v12 — Demand Planning

**Date:** 2026-07-03  
**Candidate:** `gpt-image-2-candidate-v12-brand-story-forecast-board.png`  
**Status:** candidate / not final  

## Direction

This candidate moves away from copying the Supply Chain Resilience OS layout and instead applies the same Shetty's Desk brand story to a different artifact type:

> Demand Planning Decision Board

The goal is not to repeat Ref 19 or SC OS. The goal is to make it feel like the same high-end designer created a new supply-chain artifact for a different planning problem.

## What Improved

- Same luminous Shetty's Desk palette family: white, pale blue, azure, navy, eco-green, coral.
- Different artifact architecture: decision board instead of operating-system poster.
- Topic-specific logic: signals, variance, assumptions, decision endpoints, learn loop.
- Stronger brand continuity than the rejected warm workbook direction.
- Better practical value than a generic forecast-method card.

## What Still Needs Taste Review

- Some icon treatment may still feel too literal or app-like.
- The decision endpoint cards could be more premium and less rounded-widget.
- More authored micro-detail could help it feel less like a clean diagram and more like a studio artifact.

## Reusable Learning

Brand consistency means:

- shared palette,
- shared dimensional polish,
- shared physical staging,
- shared logo authorship,
- shared practitioner density,
- shared restraint,
- but topic-specific artifact architecture.

It does not mean every post should copy the SC OS composition or use Ref 19 as the only layout reference.
