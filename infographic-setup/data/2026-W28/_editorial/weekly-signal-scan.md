## 7. Week Thesis

Supplier performance is not only about whether suppliers hit KPIs. The more useful question is:

> Which suppliers can stop the business, which ones can flex when demand moves, and which review process actually changes behavior?

That is the weekly spine. Every post should help the reader see supplier management as dependency, optionality, and accountability.
