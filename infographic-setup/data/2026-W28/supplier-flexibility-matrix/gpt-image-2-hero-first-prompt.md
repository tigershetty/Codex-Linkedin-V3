## Prompt

Create a world-class editorial LinkedIn infographic about supplier flexibility during a demand spike.

Use a hero-first composition, closer to a cinematic illustrated scene than a dashboard. The image should be dominated by one spectacular photorealistic chameleon-like supply-chain guardian, huge in the frame, crouched over a miniature logistics command table. The creature represents supplier flexibility: adaptive, alert, tactile, intelligent. It has realistic detailed skin, cobalt markings, sharp eyes, and one hand or tongue redirecting a glowing orange demand spike into controlled blue supply routes.

The lower half is a dense miniature supply-chain world: warehouses, supplier docks, pallets, forklifts, trucks, containers, route maps, factory modules, and glowing network lines. Five supplier platforms are embedded naturally in the scene, not in a rigid table. Supplier A should be visibly strongest with the brightest stable cobalt route. Supplier B and Supplier C should be useful secondary routes. Supplier D and Supplier E should look weaker, gray, constrained, or not yet verified.

Make the supplier flexibility matrix visible through annotations and route design, not a spreadsheet. Use compact labels and callout pins around the scene:
- Supplier A - Call first
- Supplier B - Backup
- Supplier C - Backup
- Supplier D - Watch risk
- Supplier E - Watch risk
- Volume swing
- Response time
- MOQ
- Constraint
- Confidence

Design style:
Premium editorial infographic, photorealistic hero object plus strong graphic annotation layer. Warm cream paper background, electric cobalt ink, thin grid discipline, restrained serif headline, crisp mono/sans labels, small cobalt icons, precise connector lines, tactile miniature logistics details, one orange accent for the demand spike. Dense, layered, professionally authored, but not cluttered.

The hero creature and logistics table should occupy most of the image. Keep the face and main gesture unobstructed. Do not create many boxed dashboard sections. Do not create salary/scope/role sections. Do not create long paragraphs. No statistics. No fake logos. No extra headers. If text becomes crowded, remove secondary labels and keep the hero scene readable. 4:5 portrait for LinkedIn.
