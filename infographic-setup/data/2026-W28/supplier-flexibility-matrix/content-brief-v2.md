## 4. Research Thesis

**Non-obvious thesis:** Supplier flexibility is not a personality trait. It is a measurable capability: volume swing, lead-time response, MOQ, allocation rules, and constraint history.  
**Counterargument / caveat:** AI can structure and compare the matrix, but only humans or supplier-confirmed data can validate true available capacity.  
**What people get wrong:** They ask "is this supplier flexible?" instead of "how much can they flex, how fast, and under what constraint?"  
**Meeting-room use:** A supply planner can bring the matrix into a demand spike or allocation meeting.

## 7. AI-for-SC Only

**Role:** Supply Planner  
**Recurring frustration:** demand changes faster than supplier capability is understood.  
**Input data required:** supplier list, current volume, confirmed capacity, lead time, MOQ, allocation constraints, recent expedite history.  
**AI workflow:** Claude structures the matrix, normalizes fields, flags missing data, ranks flexibility, and drafts supplier questions.  
**Verification step:** planner checks every capacity claim against supplier confirmation or ERP/history; no AI-invented capacity allowed.  
**When NOT to use AI:** when capacity data is missing, outdated, or commercially sensitive and not approved for the tool.  
**Reusable artifact:** supplier flexibility matrix + supplier follow-up question list.
