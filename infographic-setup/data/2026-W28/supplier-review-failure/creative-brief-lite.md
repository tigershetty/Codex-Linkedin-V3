## 4. Visual Argument

**One-sentence visual move:** Show a supplier review failing when the score dead-ends, then show the better loop where the metric turns into cause, owner, action, and checkpoint.

**Eye path:** headline -> left broken loop -> right action loop -> bottom checklist question

**Value-density layer:** Make the contrast operational: the broken side should visually dead-end after Score, while the better side carries evidence ticks, owner handoff marks, action checkpoints, and loop closure cues that make the review process feel reusable.
