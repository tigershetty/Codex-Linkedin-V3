## 3. Research Thesis

**Non-obvious thesis:** A supplier risk profile is weak if it only lists external risk signals. The profile needs dependency: what the supplier touches, what alternatives exist, and how long recovery takes.  
**Counterargument / caveat:** Gemini can surface public signals, but it cannot know internal dependency, approved alternatives, contract leverage, or actual switching time unless provided.  
**What people get wrong:** They confuse "supplier has risks" with "this supplier creates material risk for us."  
**Meeting-room use:** A category manager can use this to prepare a supplier risk review or mitigation conversation.

## 4. Content Shape

**Hook direction:** Result-First / Question-How  
**Opening line candidate:** A supplier risk profile is not complete until it answers one question: what happens to us if this supplier fails?  
**Core explanation:** Combine public signals with internal dependency. AI gathers market/geography/financial/cyber/regulatory signals. Human adds spend, part criticality, qualified alternatives, contract leverage, and recovery time.  
**Reusable takeaway:** supplier risk profile schema.  
**CTA question:** Which supplier in your base has the highest business impact, not just the highest risk score?

## 6. AI-for-SC Only

**Role:** Category Manager  
**Recurring frustration:** supplier risk profiles are either too generic or too slow to assemble.  
**Input data required:** supplier name, geography, category, spend, critical parts/services, contract expiry, approved alternatives, internal dependency notes.  
**AI workflow:** Gemini gathers public signals and drafts a profile; human adds internal dependency and verifies sources.  
**Verification step:** every public claim gets a source; every internal risk rating traces to dependency, alternative, or recovery-time evidence.  
**When NOT to use AI:** when supplier information is confidential, unsourced, or when the tool cannot access reliable current data.  
**Reusable artifact:** supplier risk profile schema + verification checklist.
