## 4. Research Thesis

**Non-obvious thesis:** Having many suppliers does not mean you have resilience. Resilience depends on qualified alternatives for the suppliers attached to critical parts, high switching cost, or long recovery time.  
**Counterargument / caveat:** Spend concentration is useful but incomplete; low-spend suppliers can be critical if they supply a unique component.  
**What people get wrong:** They rank supplier risk by spend alone.  
**Meeting-room use:** A manager can use this to ask, "Which three suppliers would stop us this quarter if they failed?"

## 5. Content Shape

**Hook direction:** Question-Why / Decision-Pressure  
**Opening line candidate:** You may have 200 suppliers in the system, but only 3 of them can stop your company.  
**Core explanation:** Supplier concentration risk is not about supplier count. It is about dependency, substitution time, and business impact.  
**Reusable takeaway:** A 3-question concentration test: critical part? qualified alternative? recovery time?  
**CTA question:** Which supplier in your current base would be hardest to replace in the next 30 days?
