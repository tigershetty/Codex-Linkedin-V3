## 3. Adaptation To Shetty's Desk

**What to borrow:** Partaker-style "complete book in one page" density; consulting-grade hierarchy; distinct mini-frameworks; strong artifact naming; source-quality thinking; save utility  
**What to avoid copying:** Partaker branding, exact strategy topic, generic business-strategy modules, repeated template grid, arbitrary source/footer clutter  
**Supply-chain translation:** turn the one-page strategy-book idea into a physical supply-chain operating artifact: supplier, port, warehouse, customer, risk radar, process modules, recovery curve  
**Tiger-specific angle:** resilience is not a slogan; it is an operating rhythm that helps teams see risk, choose a response, and learn from the disruption  
**Best-fit calendar topics:** supplier risk, demand planning, S&OP failure modes, inventory positioning, disruption response, AI planning workflows, procurement negotiation, contract risk
