## Why This Became The Benchmark

This image is the first visual in the repo that hit the intended Shetty's Desk flagship bar:

- The artifact is the hero, not a decorative scene.
- The desk and supply-chain objects give premium 3D/isometric presence without pulling attention away from the operating tool.
- The topic is obviously supply-chain-native at a glance: supplier, port, warehouse, customer, risk radar.
- The core artifact feels useful: a leader could save it before a resilience review.
- The module structure is rich enough to feel authored: orchestrate, plan, source, transform, fulfill, recover, learn, plus resilience scorecard and modes.
- The Shetty's Desk logo treatment feels like authorship when centered in the footer.

## Accepted Edit Instruction

The correct final edit preserved the previous iteration's angle, 3D depth, desk props, and logo style, then changed only:

- remove the `BUILT WITH CLAUDE` chip,
- remove the bottom review loop,
- remove the source frame/source text,
- move the same Shetty's Desk logo treatment to the center footer area.

## Failed Edit Lessons

Do not repeat these cleanup approaches:

- A flat footer band made the image feel patched and destroyed the desk angle.
- A deterministic logo replacement changed the logo feel and ignored the accepted original treatment.
- Broad clone-patch edits left visible artifacts.
- Any cleanup that redraws the whole composition risks losing the "show-stopper" quality.
