## Draft caption

When a supplier date moves, start with customer need-by.

The supplier signal matters only in relation to the customer date it could affect. First make the
customer need-by, current promise and allocation priority visible. Then test whether the supplier
signal creates a credible miss across best, base and worst scenarios. After that, test whether
recovery can genuinely close the gap.

This proposed working check puts those questions on one date-control card:

- protect customer need-by: the date, current promise and allocation context;
- test credible exposure: supplier source/reason, confirmation and scenarios;
- test recovery: stock, alternative source, split delivery or freight;
- when a credible miss remains, let KAM and Purchasing jointly own the customer exception,
  message and next update.

It is not a delivery-prediction model, and it does not remove the uncertainty. It is a way to make
the customer-first decision visible before the commitment moves.
