# Flagship Post Card — Product Geometry Shapes Network Options

**Route:** Flagship / evidence-led public operating case

**Renderer:** Image engine native (GPT Image 2).

**Reader and real work moment:** A planner, buyer, or transformation leader is reviewing global versus
regional sourcing and the conversation has started as a country-level binary instead of a
SKU-level operating decision.

**Opening tension:** A product can be efficient to ship and still need its network re-tested.

**The visible proof:** One unbranded modular product turns continuously from a compact flat-pack form
into a cubic form. Its transparent volume chamber and the connected blue/green technical field make
the illustrative network consequence visible before the caption names the commercial factors.

**The useful keep:** Product geometry shapes network options; re-test a portfolio by SKU across pack
density, landed cost, duty exposure, volatility, viable capacity, and response-time value.
