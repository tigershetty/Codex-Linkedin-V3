A supplier date moved. That does not automatically mean you have a customer exception.

The mistake is to treat every changed confirmation as if it already tells you what to promise, what freight to buy, or who should escalate.

It does not.

First, establish whether the date is credible. Then make the customer commitment visible. Only then can the team assess whether there is a real gap to recover—or an exception that needs an accountable decision.

The check is designed to keep two questions separate:

- inventing a recovery date too early;
- optimising cost before agreeing on what the customer date actually requires.

The visual is an illustrative meeting check, not a predictive system or a universal policy. If your operating model has different owners, use its logic—not its labels.

The question worth carrying into the meeting is: what fact do we still need before this customer date can honestly hold?
