## Why this is the right pilot

This is a real candidate with an observed account precedent, but its public promise remains a
bounded framework. It tests whether the previous career-map response can be made more useful by
letting a reader locate one adjacent decision language, rather than reissuing a six-path ladder.

## Required composition

- 1080 × 1350 Figma frame, exported as an exact static master;
- dark-ink title and one cobalt entry point: `Learn the next decision your work affects`;
- an asymmetric **translator atlas**, not a wheel, hierarchy, or universal function-to-function
  network: six named functions and six labelled, left-to-right decision-language transfer gates;
- only the six module phrases from the spec; no invented career, salary, outcome, or seniority claim;
- one amber/coral field note at the bottom: specialist depth still matters;
- one action: ask which decision your work needs to make easier for the next owner.
