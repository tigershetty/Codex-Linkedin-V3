# Hook options — Six Supply-Chain Translation Prompts

1. “Be more cross-functional” is useful advice until you try to decide what to learn on Monday.

2. Most cross-functional advice names the ambition, but not the next decision your work needs to support.

3. Your next supply-chain move may not be into another function. It may be into the decision your work makes possible.

4. Being good at your own function is not the same as making the next team’s decision easier.

**Selected opening:** 1

**Why:** It starts with a familiar frustration, then lets the visual supply the concrete working
reference instead of putting the whole framework in the first line.
