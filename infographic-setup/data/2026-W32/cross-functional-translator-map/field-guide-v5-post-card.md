## Reader job

An early- or mid-career supply-chain practitioner has heard “be more cross-functional” but does
not know what to learn next. The visual gives one usable move: start at the function they own,
follow one directional transfer gate, and name the decision language that would make their work
more useful to the next owner.

## Creative decision trail

The old career-map precedent established broad reader interest in self-location. The original V5
idea was corrected before production: its six links map a function to a decision language, not to
a verified receiving function, so it cannot honestly be drawn as a network. Three independent
visual reviews rejected a wheel, hierarchy, continuous spine, serial coding, and SaaS-style CTA.
The selected direction uses six independent left-to-right transfer gates, a narrow ownership
boundary, and one next-meeting question.
