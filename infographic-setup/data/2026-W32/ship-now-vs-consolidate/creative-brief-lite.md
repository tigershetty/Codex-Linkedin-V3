## 1. Reader Contract

**Audience segment:** purchasing practitioners and Purchasing Managers deciding whether a ready inbound shipment may wait for another compatible PO.  
**Audience job:** make and explain a defensible ship-now, consolidate, data-stop, review, replan, or escalation decision.  
**After reading, they can:** test the customer-date limit and economic limit in the correct order and use the smaller credible limit as the maximum permitted hold.  
**Meeting/task/career moment:** a ready shipment is awaiting release while another PO may soon become available for consolidation.  
**Care statement:** freight savings can remain positive while a credible arrival scenario misses the customer need-by date, so the buyer needs a rule that protects the date without hiding the economics.  
**Opening claim:** A freight saving can be perfectly real and still be the wrong shipping decision.
