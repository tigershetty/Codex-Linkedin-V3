# Direction 04 — Time-to-Wait Tableau

**Status:** historical native candidate. V12 is retained as the precursor that established the
time-constraint lesson; it is superseded for C3 by `05-counterfactual-dispatch-diorama.md` and
`visual-v14-counterfactual-dispatch-diorama-linkedin-4x5.png`.

## The visual wager

Can a reader understand the actual decision without a control board, a panel stack, or an HTML
overlay? The image must make one invisible operating constraint—the last safe departure—physically
visible and show how a supplier-ready range may cross it.

## Reader promise

`"Ready tomorrow" is not a supply commitment.`

The packed PO can wait only when the **latest credible** second-PO date remains inside the
customer-safe wait window. A lower combined freight rate is deliberately not the hero.

## One native-image composition

- **Headline:** `READY TOMORROW?`
- **Editorial deck:** `Can the customer date afford the wait?`
- **Scene:** a luminous, museum-quality miniature freight departure landscape on a pale blueprint
  field. A packed PO is ready to leave; a translucent second-PO date envelope has an uncertain
  coral edge; one vertical, transparent last-safe-departure plane creates the end of a green wait
  window; the customer need-by is a concrete wall or terminal beyond it.
- **Causal proof:** the credible readiness envelope visibly overlaps/crosses the departure plane.
  The reader sees that the expected date is not enough.
- **Maximum hero labels:** `READY NOW`, `READY RANGE`, `LAST SAFE DEPARTURE`, `CUSTOMER NEED-BY`.
- **No:** decision board, card grid, lower takeaway strip, footer, generated logo, output buttons,
  interface panels, decorative machinery, or generic control-room chrome.

## Acceptance test and result

With the headline covered, a planner should still see within ten seconds that waiting for PO B
becomes unsafe when its latest credible readiness crosses the customer-safe departure boundary.

**Historical result:** V12 made range → boundary → release visible, but did not make the
consolidation counterfactual or the two loads sufficiently tangible. It is preserved as a learning
artifact rather than the active candidate.

**Render ledger:** V11 first native render proved the scene but not the causal range crossing. V12
corrected that constraint, then C3 moved to a different declared visual primitive—Counterfactual
Dispatch Diorama—to make the actual consolidation choice visible. That later family has its own
bounded V13 → V14 ledger in Direction 05.
