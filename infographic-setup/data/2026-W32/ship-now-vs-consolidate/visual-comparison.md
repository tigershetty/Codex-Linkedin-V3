## Final Decision

**Chosen lane:** hybrid editorial illustration plus deterministic finishing.

The generated image is restricted to the no-text physical metaphor. Every load-bearing word, formula, relationship, scope note and identity element is rendered deterministically, which keeps the creative richness without handing factual or brand control to the image model.

`visual.png` and `visual-linkedin.png` are exact copies of the inspected `html-control.png` export. The full-size asset and the `qa/mobile-preview.png` reduction were visually checked after the decision-strip labels were reflowed.
