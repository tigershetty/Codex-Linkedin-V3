## Reader contract

- **3 seconds:** a ready order can either leave or wait, and the customer need-by controls whether
  waiting is defensible.
- **10 seconds:** green shows the customer-safe direct route; coral shows the uncertain wait path;
  the three outcome cards make the next move visible.
- **30 seconds:** retain the customer-safe-window rule and use the caption for the decision order,
  uncertainty, and exception governance.

## Integrity and creative checks

- [x] One human-operating question and one central route-switch scene.
- [x] No synthetic dates, costs, outcomes, customer proof, or calculator fixture appears.
- [x] Blue describes the system; green means protected customer date; coral means uncertainty/risk.
- [x] Exact overlay text, footer logo, and decision cards are deterministic.
- [x] The visual does not rely on the caption to explain the basic tension.
- [x] The caption provides complementary reasoning rather than reading the image aloud.
- [x] Cobalt Grid treatment is not used.

## Remaining approval question

Does the headline and illustration make a working purchaser immediately recognise a real release
decision, rather than feel that they are being shown another supply-chain infographic? If not, kill
or rework this direction before any further polishing.
