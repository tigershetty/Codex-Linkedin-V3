# Hook Routes — C3 V8

**Status:** Draft. Selected after the V3 native decision-board test; final Tiger approval pending.

## Primary hook

> Your supplier says the second PO will be ready tomorrow. The first is packed, and the combined
> freight rate is lower if you wait. Does the customer date give you room to hold it?

## Alternatives

1. The cheaper rate answers a different question: not whether you still have time to wait.
2. A ready PO and a lower combined freight rate create a tempting decision. The customer date
   decides whether that temptation is real.
3. Before holding a ready shipment for consolidation, find the departure you cannot afford to miss.
4. Supplier "tomorrow" is not the same thing as a customer-safe release date.

## Selection note

The primary opens with a live, recognisable work moment and asks for the missing variable. It does
not announce a lesson, invent a personal story, or make the visual repeat its own conclusion.
