# Hook routes — C3 V6

## Selected

**The freight quote says wait. The last safe departure says ship.**

The hook puts two valid signals in direct conflict, but it also names the operational mechanism that
resolves the conflict. The visual makes the customer date tangible through the `LAST SAFE
DEPARTURE` gate rather than asking the reader to take a generic customer-first statement on trust.

## Alternatives

1. **“Ready tomorrow” is not a release date.**
2. **The customer date is not a freight-saving buffer.**
3. **A combined rate can be cheaper and still be the wrong release decision.**

## Visual hierarchy

The lower-freight tag is a real physical pull against the ready shipment. The gate, green route,
and customer dock carry the customer promise without a lower information strip. The caption adds
the scenario test and joint exception ownership; it does not translate the picture.
