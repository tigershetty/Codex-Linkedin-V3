## 1. Reader and Work Moment

**Primary reader:** purchasing practitioner or Purchasing Manager.  
**Work moment:** shipment A is ready, shipment B may be ready shortly, and the buyer must decide whether A may wait for consolidation.  
**Required partner:** the KAM becomes a decision partner when the customer commitment is exposed; this role wording remains pending public-use approval.  
**Why the reader cares:** freight savings can remain positive even when a credible arrival scenario misses the customer need-by date. The buyer needs a decision that is fast, explainable, and defensible.

## 2. Reader Contract

**3 seconds:** A ready shipment is waiting for a second PO—but the customer date may not permit the hold.  
**10 seconds:** The maximum defensible hold is the smaller of the service-safe limit and the economic break-even limit across every credible scenario.  
**30 seconds:** Check compatibility and input freshness, calculate both limits, then ship, consolidate, stop, review, or escalate with the reason recorded.

**Standalone LinkedIn payoff:** the reader receives the complete decision order, two-limit formula, and failure boundary without clicking away.  
**Website payoff:** the calculator applies the same rule to current dates, ranges, quotes, and differential costs and exports an audit record.

## 3. Historical Creative Direction

**Direction (historical):** `The Last Safe Departure`
**Opening tension:** the freight quote can reward waiting while the customer date still requires departure today.  
**Dominant object:** one cobalt route switch that sends a ready pallet toward either `SHIP NOW` or `WAIT + CONSOLIDATE`; both paths terminate at one customer need-by gate.  
**Reading route:** decision scene -> best/base/worst timing band -> service-safe hold -> economic break-even hold -> smaller limit -> decision state.  
**Useful object:**  

```text
permitted hold_s = min(service-safe hold_s, economic break-even hold_s)
robust permitted hold = min(permitted hold_s across all credible scenarios)
```

**Visual boundary:** do not make the calculator UI, a synthetic scenario, a dashboard, or a generic side-by-side comparison the hero.  
**Caption job:** define the inputs and decision sequence, explain false precision and exceptions, and establish what the visual deliberately compresses.
