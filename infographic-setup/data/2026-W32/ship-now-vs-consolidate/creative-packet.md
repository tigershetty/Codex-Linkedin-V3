## Reader Contract

- **Reader:** purchasing practitioners and Purchasing Managers deciding whether a ready inbound shipment may wait for another compatible PO.
- **Job:** make and explain a defensible ship-now, consolidate, data-stop, review, replan, or escalation decision.
- **Work moment:** a ready shipment is awaiting release while another PO may soon become available for consolidation.
- **Why they care:** freight savings can remain positive while a credible arrival scenario misses the customer need-by date, so the buyer needs a rule that protects the date without hiding the economics.
- **3 seconds:** A ready shipment is waiting for a second PO, but the customer date may not permit the hold.
- **10 seconds:** The maximum defensible hold is the smaller of the service-safe limit and the economic break-even limit across every credible scenario.
- **30 seconds:** Check compatibility and input freshness, calculate both limits, then ship, consolidate, stop, review, or escalate with the reason recorded.
