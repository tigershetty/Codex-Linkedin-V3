Your supplier says the second PO will be ready tomorrow. The first is packed, and the combined
freight rate is lower if you wait. Does the customer date give you room to hold it?

My view is that the freight saving is a valid input, but it cannot be the first one. Start with the
customer need-by and work backwards to the last departure that still protects it; then put the
second PO's credible ready-date range against that point.

If the full credible range clears that boundary, there is room to consolidate. If one credible
scenario does not—and the packed PO can still protect the customer date—release the packed PO.

The harder case is when neither route protects service. That needs to be visible and owned by the
KAM and Purchasing Manager together, rather than becoming a quiet workaround inside planning.

That does not remove the freight quote from the decision. It puts the quote where it belongs: after
the customer commitment has been checked.
