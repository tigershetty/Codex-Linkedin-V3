native editorial teaching instrument, not a warehouse photo, SaaS UI, generic dashboard, card
collage, poster, or HTML assembly.

Make the reader's decision visible in one physical **RELEASE DECISION BOARD** on a luminous white
blueprint drafting surface. Use a large native headline: `SHIP NOW OR CONSOLIDATE?` and the
subhead: `THE CHEAPER RATE ONLY WINS IF THE CUSTOMER DATE STILL CLEARS.` The inputs are integral
engineering tiles labelled `READY PO`, `SECOND PO: DATE RANGE`, and `CUSTOMER NEED-BY`, with
physical cobalt connectors running into a calibrated `LAST SAFE DEPARTURE` gate.

protected path is labelled `IF RANGE MISSES` then `SHIP NOW`; a cobalt permitted path is labelled
`IF RANGE CLEARS` then `CONSOLIDATE` with a small green verification cue. Coral appears only around
the uncertain second-PO date range and the at-risk interval. Include one compact, integrated
decision order: `1 CUSTOMER DATE → 2 SAFE DEPARTURE → 3 SUPPLIER RANGE → 4 FREIGHT RATE`.

Use brushed aluminium, cobalt anodising, sapphire acrylic, engraved native typography, real
fasteners, precise cable routes, blue/green/coral semantics, and V3-level editorial hierarchy.
Render every word, label, connector, and component as one image-engine-native composition. Do not
invent numbers, company proof, outcomes, or extra copy; do not add a logo, footer, watermark, people,
or tool chips.
