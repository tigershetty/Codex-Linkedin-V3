# Hook routes — C3 V5

## Selected

**The quote says wait. The customer date says ship.**

Two true signals are pulling in opposite directions. It is specific enough for a planner, buyer, or
KAM to recognise immediately, while leaving the physical visual to show what the decision costs.

## Alternatives

1. **“Ready tomorrow” is not a release date.**
2. **A better freight rate is not always a better shipment.**
3. **The truck is ready. The second PO is not. Who owns the wait?**

## Visual hierarchy

The freight quote is a real physical pull, not a sidebar of numbers. A loaded truck faces the
customer-safe lane and the last safe departure gate while a coral lower-freight tag pulls it
backwards. The caption supplies the method and the exception ownership; the image must not repeat
the rule in a second explanatory panel.
