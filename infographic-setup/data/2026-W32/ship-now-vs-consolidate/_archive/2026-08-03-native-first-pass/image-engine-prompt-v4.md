# Image-engine prompt — V4 native render

Use case: infographic-diagram

Asset type: LinkedIn 4:5 editorial visual, 1080 by 1350 or taller vertical ratio.

Primary request: Create an original Shetty's Desk Operating Studio visual about a planner deciding
whether to hold a shipment that is ready now because a second purchase order may be ready tomorrow.
The visual must tell the story before any caption is read.

Scene and visual argument: Show a bright, premium 2.5D isometric outbound warehouse and loading
dock from a high three-quarter view. On the left, one fully packed pallet is physically ready next
to a blue delivery truck. Its lane passes toward a literal industrial departure gate in the middle.
Behind a translucent coral timing canopy, a second smaller pallet is visibly not ready yet. The
gate is the decisive object: its green lamp is on, the ready pallet can still pass, but a coral
red timing corridor begins just beyond it. On the right, a customer receiving dock and its open
delivery window are visibly protected only if the ready shipment leaves now.

Native typography within the scene: Use only four short, clear, correctly spelled all-capital
phrases, printed on physical signs or integrated title treatment: CAN WE WAIT? at the top;
READY NOW near the left pallet; LAST SAFE DEPARTURE on the central gate; CUSTOMER NEED-BY on the
right dock. Do not include a paragraph, dashboard labels, a legend, or small cards.

Style and composition: V3 Operating Studio language, luminous white and icy-blue background, azure
and navy infrastructure, glass and brushed aluminium surfaces, deep soft shadows, clean high-end
3D illustration, generous spatial depth, crisp readable large type, practical warehouse detail.
Green means the customer-safe path; coral means time risk; blue means the operating system. The
eye moves left to right from ready shipment, through the gate, to the customer promise.

Constraints: Do not use HTML-style panels, pasted cards, browser UI, a generic SaaS dashboard,
stock-photo people, mascots, watermark, or any brand logo. Do not imitate the exact composition,
wording, or artwork of a saved reference. Keep the entire result as one coherent image-engine
rendered composition.
