# Hook Routes — C3 V9 Framework Blind Spot

**Status:** Historical hook set — superseded by `hooks-v10-temporal-consolidation.md`; retained for learning only.

## Recommended hook

> A freight quote gives you a number. “Ready tomorrow” gives you an assumption.

## Alternatives

1. The lower combined rate only matters after the supplier-ready range clears the customer-safe
   departure.
2. A ready PO should not become the buffer for an uncertain supplier promise.
3. Before holding a ready shipment, find the departure the customer date cannot afford to lose.
4. The freight saving becomes a decision input only after service feasibility has cleared.

## Selection note

The visual already makes the range cross the departure boundary. The hook supplies the blind spot
behind that picture: a precise quote can sit beside an imprecise supplier promise. It is a proposed
framework, not a reported event, outcome, or personal story.
