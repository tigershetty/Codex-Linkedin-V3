**Status:** Draft — final Tiger approval and publication pending

One PO is packed. The second is "ready tomorrow," and the combined freight rate is lower.

Would you hold the first shipment?

The saving may be real, but the first thing to establish is the last departure that still protects
the customer need-by date.

Then test the second PO against a credible ready-date range — not the most optimistic "tomorrow."
If that range clears the departure, consolidate. If it does not and the packed PO still protects the
date, release the packed PO.

And if neither route protects service, it is no longer a quiet planner call. The KAM and Purchasing
Manager should own the exception together: replan, expedite, or knowingly accept the service risk.

The rate matters. It just cannot decide the date on its own.
