## 4. Research Thesis

**Non-obvious thesis:** The executive S&OP meeting is not where functional analysis should begin. It is where a small number of already-structured cross-functional trade-offs are decided.
**Counterargument / caveat:** Review names, sequence, and ownership vary by company and maturity. The invariant is that product, demand, supply, and financial implications are reconciled before executives are asked to approve one plan.
**What people get wrong:** They count the executive meeting as pre-work, treat finance as a final validation slide, or escalate issues without an owner, feasible options, and a clear ask.
**Meeting-room use:** Use the five review contracts to inspect the monthly calendar, clarify ownership, and reject incomplete issues before they consume executive time.
