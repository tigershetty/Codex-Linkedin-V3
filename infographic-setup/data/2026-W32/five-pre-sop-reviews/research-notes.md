# Research Notes - The Five Pre-S&OP Reviews

**Research date:** 2026-07-19
**Purpose:** Establish an accurate, practitioner-friendly five-review chain that ends before the executive S&OP decision meeting.

## Source Findings

### ASCM - S&OP process

ASCM describes forecasting, demand planning, supply planning, a pre-S&OP meeting, and an executive S&OP meeting. It states that executives review the plans, forecasts, and recommendations produced by pre-S&OP. This supports the core distinction that executive S&OP should receive prepared recommendations rather than begin the reconciliation work.

Source: [ASCM - Sales and Operations Planning](https://www.ascm.org/topics/sales-and-operations-planning/)

### SAP - S&OP process

SAP describes workspace preparation, demand planning, supply planning, plan review and reconciliation, and finalization/release. It also notes that S&OP steps vary by organization. This supports a separate readiness review and the caveat that names and ownership should not be presented as universal.

Source: [SAP - What is Sales and Operations Planning?](https://www.sap.com/resources/sop-sales-and-operations-planning)

### SAP Learning - product through executive decision

SAP Learning presents Product Review, Demand Review, Supply Review, Demand-Supply Balancing, and Executive Decision. Product review includes lifecycle and portfolio changes; demand review establishes the consensus demand plan and scenarios; supply review tests critical resources and scaling options.

Source: [SAP Learning - Analyzing Demand Planning](https://learning.sap.com/courses/exploring-end-to-end-business-processes-in-sap-business-suite-es/analyzing-demand-planning_c51b50fc-7ec6-43e9-96e0-5842d1122d4d)

### Oliver Wight - Integrated Reconciliation

Oliver Wight describes Portfolio, Demand, and Supply reviews followed by Integrated Reconciliation. Integrated Reconciliation filters unresolved issues, financializes the operational plan, identifies gaps to annual and strategic commitments, and prepares recommended actions for executive decision.

Source: [Oliver Wight - The Integrated Reconciliation Review](https://oliverwight-eame.com/deploying-strategy-with-integrated-business-planning-the-integrated-reconciliation-review/)

## Operating Synthesis

The visual uses five pre-work reviews followed by a separate executive decision:

| Review | Lead | What it must settle | Required hand-off |
|---|---|---|---|
| Data + Performance Readiness | S&OP Lead + Analytics | actuals, gaps, assumptions | trusted exception pack |
| Portfolio Review | Product / Portfolio | launches, phase-outs, lifecycle | approved portfolio changes |
| Demand Review | Commercial / Demand | baseline, events, range | consensus demand + scenarios |
| Supply Review | Operations / Supply | capacity, materials, inventory | feasible response + options |
| Integrated Reconciliation | S&OP Lead + Finance | gaps, trade-offs, value | recommendation + decision asks |
| Executive S&OP | Executive team | scenario, priorities, resources | one committed plan |

## Accuracy Decisions

- Do not call this the only valid S&OP sequence. Use `a practical operating model` in the caption.
- Do not present Finance as a late sign-off step. Finance co-owns reconciliation and financializes options throughout the cycle.
- Do not count Executive S&OP as one of the five pre-work reviews.
- Do not imply that every issue must reach executives. Reconciliation should resolve what can be resolved and escalate only the trade-offs requiring executive authority.
- Do not use invented performance statistics.

## Readiness Rule

`READY = EVIDENCE + OWNER + OPTIONS + ASK`

This is a Shetty's Desk operating synthesis, not a quoted industry standard. It expresses the minimum structure an unresolved issue needs before it deserves executive time.
