# Direction C — The SKU Passport

**Role:** utility — not selected

- **First-frame hook:** one product passport carries the physical and commercial “visa” conditions
  that permit a SKU to travel globally or need regional response.
- **Working mechanism:** the reader follows a short passport inspection through cube, landed cost,
  duty, volatility, available capacity, and response-time value.
- **Useful keep:** a concise SKU review checklist.
- **Why it is not selected:** clear but too close to a premium framework card; it gives up the
  strong public-story visual and is better as a future website field guide.
- **Kill condition:** kill if it becomes a multi-card dashboard or a generic travel metaphor.
