# Direction B — Network Hinge Sculpture

**Role:** visual metaphor — not selected

- **First-frame hook:** a precision cobalt hinge holds one flat product between a vast global reach
  and a tighter local response arc.
- **Working mechanism:** five calibrated forces pull the hinge: cube, cost, duty, volatility,
  response. The product can only settle into one stable operating geometry once the forces are
  considered together.
- **Useful keep:** a sourcing decision is a balancing act across a SKU's actual trade-offs, not a
  map-colour decision.
- **Why it is not selected:** it risks becoming an elegant but generic kinetic sculpture; the
  forensic carton gives the public evidence and product/network relationship a more specific home.
- **Kill condition:** kill if removing the product leaves a generic balance metaphor.
