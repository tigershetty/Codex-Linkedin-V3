## Reader contract

**Reader:** a planner, buyer, or transformation leader entering a global-versus-regional sourcing conversation.

**First-frame proposition:** one source country can create several different network decisions when the physical and commercial demands of the SKUs differ.

**Useful keep:** before making a country-level decision, compare each SKU against pack density, total landed cost, duty/trade exposure, volatility, credible capacity, and response-time value.

## Final visual

**Asset:** `visual-v4-one-source-country-five-network-decisions.png`

One shared brushed-metal source hub sends five visibly different, unbranded SKU specimens through five physical route fields. Blue indicates system reach, green a viable/protected option, and coral a short constrained route ending at a physical fence. The title names the decision without becoming a separate card or overlay:

> ONE SOURCE COUNTRY. FIVE NETWORK DECISIONS.

The visual is a decision illustration, not a map, a cost model, or an IKEA network diagram.
