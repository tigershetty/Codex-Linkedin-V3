## Prompt

Use case: `scientific-educational`
Asset type: LinkedIn flagship visual for Shetty's Desk

Create a premium 4:5 technical-editorial cover, entirely image-native. Integrate large, clean,
readable navy and cobalt title typography into the upper fold of the composition: **“THE PRODUCT
DESIGNS THE NETWORK”**.

On a luminous ice-white technical drawing table, show one unbranded flat-pack shelving/cabinet
product as a museum-grade exploded technical plate: raw birch panels, cobalt anodised fittings,
brushed aluminium, clear acrylic, fine blueprint registration marks, and long diffused studio
shadows. The engineering sheet underneath is also a physical folded network atlas.

On the left, thin nested panels pass through a transparent acrylic cube-test chamber and continue
as one elegant narrow cobalt long-distance route. On the right, a bulky cabinet-volume silhouette
physically expands the same folded map into a closer protected green source radius. A single thin
coral cost-pressure seam crosses both routes and visibly changes their feasible path without
numbers. The product geometry must visibly create the network geometry.

Engrave only this small working rule into the central cobalt fold-spine: **“PACK DENSITY → LANDED
COST → SOURCE RADIUS”**.

High-key white and blue Operating Studio, precise 2.5D/isometric depth, calm editorial hierarchy,
tactile industrial-design realism, one central visual spine. It must feel like a world-class
industrial-design magazine cover and a planner's useful instrument at once. Use a source-free,
unbranded product: this is not an IKEA visual and it does not depict any company’s exact network.

Do not include logos, flags, cartons, shipping containers, pallets, factory/conveyor scenes,
generic globe clip-art, dashboard cards, fake charts, numerical claims, extra labels, footer, or
watermark. Do not use dark gunmetal, video-game, or product-advertising art direction. If the small
engraved rule cannot be rendered cleanly, omit it rather than inventing words.
