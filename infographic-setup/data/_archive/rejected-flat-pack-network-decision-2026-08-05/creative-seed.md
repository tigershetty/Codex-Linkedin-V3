# Creative Seed — The Flat-Pack Paradox

**Work moment:** A planner, buyer, or transformation leader is asked whether a product should stay
in a global network or move closer to the customer. The discussion starts with a generic country
decision instead of the physical and commercial properties of the individual SKU.

**Creative proposition:** An unbranded flat-pack furniture carton literally unfolds into two
different supply-network architectures. The product is not sitting beside the network; its geometry
is creating the network economics.

**Reader payoff:** Replace the binary question “should we nearshore?” with “which SKU has changed
enough that its global network needs to be re-tested?”

**Signature detail:** the central carton fold is also a calibrated network hinge. The physical fold
determines whether the reader sees a long-haul container route or a nearby responsive production
route. It is a working metaphor, not a decorative split screen.

**No-copy boundary:** No IKEA logo, recognisable product, yellow-blue branding, copied creator
layout, generic pallet/conveyor scene, fake chart, or unsupported number.
