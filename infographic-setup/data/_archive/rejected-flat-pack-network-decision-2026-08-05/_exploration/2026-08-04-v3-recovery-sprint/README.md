# V3 Recovery Sprint — 2026-08-04

**Purpose:** Test three title-free, image-engine-native visual arguments against the same
evidence-backed product-geometry story. These are rough direction tiles, not publication candidates.

## The recovered brand rule

Shetty's Desk is a visual field journal of hidden operating decisions. The image must perform a
specific physical verb before a caption or headline explains it: a decision variable must visibly
change the object, route, boundary, or consequence.

## Direction set

1. **Freight Shadow** — empty volume becomes a material freight consequence.
2. **One Origin, Many Networks** — identical origin; distinct SKUs create different viable network
   radii.
3. **Dimensional Threshold** — a product form physically crosses or fails a product-specific
   long-distance test.

## Fast-fail test

Kill a direction unless, at thumbnail size, it answers all three:

1. What is the editorial verdict?
2. What specific object or evidence makes it visible?
3. What mental object can the reader keep?

No direction may use a generic warehouse, pallet, container, conveyor, clock, route map, dashboard,
title bar, or external overlay to explain itself. A selected direction receives at most one named
correction; the other two remain documented learning artefacts.

## Decision

- **01 Freight Shadow:** killed — an attractive folding/product test did not prove the network
  decision.
- **03 Dimensional Threshold:** killed — an attractive compression rig required explanatory copy
  before its operational meaning resolved.
- **02 One Origin, Many Networks:** selected conditionally, then corrected once in
  `04-one-origin-many-networks-boundary.png`. The fifth physical SKU and its short coral boundary
  made constrained viable reach legible rather than decorative.

The selected direction entered final production as
`../../visual-v4-one-source-country-five-network-decisions.png`. The final native title is a
production treatment, not a further direction iteration: it names the already-visible proposition.
The untitled corrected render is retained here as
`05-one-origin-many-networks-clean-untitled.png` for learning, not publication.
