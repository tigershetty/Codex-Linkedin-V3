# Creative Brief Lite — The Network Fold

**Status:** One bounded corrective render authorised after V1 failed the 10-second causal-read test.

## Reader contract

**Reader:** planner, purchasing practitioner, or transformation leader reviewing a global versus
regional sourcing question.
**First-frame proposition:** product geometry shapes the network options.
**Useful keep:** a compact form and an assembled cubic form can have different viable network
options; assess the rest of the SKU context before deciding.

## Visual argument

One unbranded modular product is a **continuous folding cutaway**, not an exploded product beside a
separate map. Its compact flat-panel state passes through a narrow clear-acrylic volume chamber and
continues directly into a long cobalt route. Along the same physical hinge, the panels unfold into
an assembled cubic state; its expanded volume is caught by a larger transparent chamber and the
route visibly contracts into a nearer green sourcing field. The one object is the causal trace.

The product is the network map. This is not an IKEA illustration, a global-versus-local split
screen, a literal network diagram, or a generic logistics scene.

## Native text

- Title: `PRODUCT GEOMETRY SHAPES NETWORK OPTIONS`
- No required secondary rule or microcopy.

If the title becomes garbled, kill the render; do not repair it with an external title/footer
overlay.

## Art direction

- **Expression:** forensic operating atlas / authored editorial engineering.
- **Format:** LinkedIn 4:5, high-key ice-white technical studio, premium 2.5D/isometric depth.
- **Materials:** raw birch panels, cobalt anodised fittings, brushed aluminium, clear acrylic,
  faint blueprint marks, long diffused studio shadows.
- **Semantic colour:** cobalt = system and long route; green = viable nearer source radius; coral =
  cost/volatility pressure. Colour must carry meaning, never decorate.
- **Composition:** title → compact product form → visible continuous hinge → cubic product form →
  directly changed routes. One reading route, no cards, grids, footer, logo, or watermark.

## Claim integrity

The visual illustrates a decision lens. It contains no named company, logo, container count,
tariff, unit, production-volume, price, or outcome claim. The caption alone carries the sourced
IKEA context and explicitly limits the generalisation.

## Fail-fast review

V1 is retained as `visual-v1-network-fold.png` and rejected as the active visual: it had a premium
material world but separated the product, the map, and the causal mechanism too much to earn the
headline. Kill V2 if it becomes a furniture advert, IKEA imitation, generic warehouse scene,
miniature logistics world, dashboard, dark product render, or an attractive object whose causal
paths do not visibly change with product geometry.
