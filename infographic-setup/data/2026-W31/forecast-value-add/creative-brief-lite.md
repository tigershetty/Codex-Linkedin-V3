## 5A. AI-for-SC Caption Promise

**Input pack the reader can gather:** Item-location, period, actual, baseline forecast, adjusted forecast, forecast version/horizon, reason code, evidence note, owner, and optional value or volume weight.  
**Tool output artifact:** FVA scorecard, override-value register, reason-code summary, harmful-override exception list, and demand-review brief.  
**Meeting value:** Start with which override patterns earned their place, which added no gain, and which made the forecast worse.  
**Boundary to keep in the brief:** Calculations require matched horizons, frozen versions, actuals, and a declared error metric. The planner owns changes to policy and the system of record.  
**Published-caption stance:** value-first.
