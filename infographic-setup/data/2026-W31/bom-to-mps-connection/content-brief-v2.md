## 4. Research Thesis

**Non-obvious thesis:** MPS quantity multiplied through the BOM creates gross component requirements, but inventory netting and lead-time offset are what turn the explosion into a useful material plan  
**Counterargument / caveat:** real MRP also uses BOM versions, lot sizing, work calendars, yield/scrap, safety stock, and other item settings; the worked example is deliberately simplified and labeled illustrative  
**What people get wrong:** the BOM is said to tell the factory what to make, or MPS + BOM are treated as if they directly create actual purchase orders  
**Meeting-room use:** trace the component and timing impact before accepting an MPS quantity/date change
