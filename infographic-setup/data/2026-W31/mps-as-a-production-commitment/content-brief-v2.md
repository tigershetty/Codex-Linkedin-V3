## 4. Research Thesis

**Non-obvious thesis:** the MPS is useful because it creates a shared item-time commitment, not because it is another planning report  
**Counterargument / caveat:** protected does not mean impossible to change; time fences and firming behavior vary by ERP and operating policy  
**What people get wrong:** forecast, MPS, detailed sequencing, freeze, and firming are treated as interchangeable  
**Meeting-room use:** test a near-term demand change against the item, quantity, period, status, capacity, material, and customer impact
