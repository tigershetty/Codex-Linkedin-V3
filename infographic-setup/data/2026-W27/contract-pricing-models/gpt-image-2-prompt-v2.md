## Framework Lock

**Framework:** The Shifting Boundary

One repeated horizontal contract bar appears three times. Each bar is split between **SUPPLIER** and **BUYER**. The only thing that changes is the vertical boundary line.

- Fixed-Price: boundary sits near the buyer side; supplier absorbs most overrun risk.
- Time & Materials: boundary sits near the middle; risk is shared but leans buyer.
- Cost-Plus: boundary sits near the supplier side; buyer absorbs most overrun risk.

The dominant element is the boundary line moving across three identical bars.
