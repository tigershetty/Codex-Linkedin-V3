Supply Agreement 6 load-bearing clauses
1
Scope & spec Top dispute source Exactly what's being bought — specs, quantities, where one side's job ends.
Vague scope is the single biggest dispute driver — cited by ~3 in 4 teams. No fixed reference for "late" or "defective."
