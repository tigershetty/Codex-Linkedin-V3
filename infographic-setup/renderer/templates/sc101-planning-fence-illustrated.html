Frozen
Now → Wk 2
Can you change it?
No — it's committed. Materials issued, machines loaded.
Cost of a change
Very high — overtime, expedite fees, a missed promise somewhere.
Who can approve one
Plant manager — and only for a real emergency.
Slushy
Wk 2 → Wk 6
Can you change it?
Yes — with trade-offs. Swap one order in, another moves out.
Cost of a change
Moderate — re-planning effort, maybe a material or capacity squeeze.
Who can approve one
Planner, inside agreed rules of the planning fence.
Liquid
Wk 6 → Wk 12+
Can you change it?
Yes — freely. Nothing is locked, so adjust at will.
Cost of a change
Low to none — you're still moving numbers, not materials.
Who can approve one
Demand & supply planning — this is where the real decisions belong.
The rule: make the decision while it's still liquid, commit it in the slushy zone, and protect the frozen zone like a promise — because that's exactly what it is.
Most "urgent" changes in the frozen zone aren't emergencies. They're decisions someone avoided making while the plan was still flexible.
