The cheapest quote is rarely
the cheapest supplier

Price is one column on a scorecard that has five. Stack them up and the lowest bid is almost never the tallest once you count what it's actually worth.
Total value = Price+ Lead time+ Quality+ Payment terms+ References
Supplier A wins on price and lands mid-table on everything else. Supplier C is cheap and weak — a false economy. Supplier B costs a little more per unit and is worth far more. That height gap is the decision.
