Four kinds of spend.
Four ways to buy.

Plot everything you buy on two questions — how much it moves your profit, and how hard it is to source. Where each thing lands tells you whether to squeeze on price, lock in supply, or stop spending attention on it. That map is the Kraljic matrix.
Ask two questions about anything you buy
YDoes it really move our profit? Big spend or critical to the product = high.
XHow hard is it to replace the supplier? Few sources or long lead time = high risk.
…and you land in one of four playbooks
Strategic → partner & protect
Leverage → push on price
Bottleneck → secure supply
Routine → automate & forget
Most teams negotiate hardest on the easy, low-value buys and barely watch the one part that could stop the line. The matrix flips that: spend your attention where the stakes and the risk are both high.
