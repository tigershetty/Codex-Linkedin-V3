The clause you fight hardest
is rarely the one that bites.

A supply contract is six load-bearing clauses. Teams burn their energy on the "if it all blows up" clauses. The disputes land somewhere else entirely.

Teams fight hardest over the clauses that insure against disaster. The disputes land on the ones that define what "done" means. Before you sign, read the clause nobody argued over.
