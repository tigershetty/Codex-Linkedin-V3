The contract looks standard.
The risk is in the clauses.
The 5 clauses a buyer should flag before signing a supplier contract, surfaced in 5 minutes using Claude.
Supplier Agreement — flaggedfirst-pass risk review
1
Price escalation
"Prices may be revised on 30 days' written notice."
Price riskNo cap, no index. They can raise prices after you've designed them in.
2
Limitation of liability
"Liability limited to the value of the affected order; consequential damages excluded."
Liability riskTheir part stops your line — you carry the downstream cost, not them.
3
Minimum volume commitment
"Buyer commits to a minimum annual purchase; shortfall billed at full price."
Commitment riskDemand drops, you still pay. Take-or-pay with a friendlier name.
4
Auto-renewal
"Renews for successive 12-month terms unless cancelled 90 days before expiry."
Lock-in riskMiss the 90-day window and you're committed for another year.
5
Soft delivery terms
"Delivery dates are estimates; no liability for delays beyond reasonable control."
Delivery riskLead times aren't binding. Late deliveries carry no penalty.
