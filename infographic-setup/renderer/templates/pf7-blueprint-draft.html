Copy-paste prompt · paste into Claude, fill the brackets
"You are a senior category manager. Draft a complete enterprise RFQ for [category], annual volume [units across 3 tiers].
Include: (1) scope & BOM with part numbers and drawings; (2) technical spec to [IATF 16949 / ISO 9001], tolerances and materials; (3) quality, PPAP Level 3, FAIR, RoHS/REACH, conflict-minerals declaration; (4) commercial terms, unit + tooling/NRE, Incoterms [DDP], [Net 60], price-break tiers, 90-day validity; (5) capacity & continuity, committed capacity, ±20% flex, BCP, dual-source; (6) ESG, Scope 3 data + supplier code with audit rights; (7) submission, sealed bid via [SAP Ariba], Q&A window, deadline.
Then build a TCO-weighted scoring matrix: TCO 30, Quality 20, Delivery 18, Technical 15, Financial 10, ESG 7.
Output (a) a supplier-ready RFQ document and (b) the scoring table to rank every bid."
Claude drafts to the standard. You own the spec and the weights. They encode your category strategy and your firm's risk tolerance, so a category manager signs off before the event goes live.
