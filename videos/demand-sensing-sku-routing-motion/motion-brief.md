## 1. Motion Job

**Motion helps this post because:**
The reader needs to see the input pack feed the routing gate, the five tests scan, and the portfolio split into frequent sensing, monthly planning, or special-method review in the same order the policy decision works.

**Picture-first promise:**
The complete approved still remains the first and final frame. Motion adds reading order without replacing the infographic.

**Motion archetype:**
`decision map + branching process + annotated hero`

## 2. Locked Elements

- complete approved background and 3D switchyard
- headline, subheading, and every body label
- Claude mark and tool chip geometry
- Shetty's Desk logo and footer placard
- all base arrows, rails, shadows, props, and panel geometry
- route meanings and the sensing/monthly/exception distinction

## 4. Reading Order

1. Hold the complete visual.
2. Pulse demand history, orders/POS, promotion/lifecycle, and lead-time/service inputs.
3. Carry the combined signal through Claude into the routing gate.
4. Scan regularity, variability, volume/value, signal freshness, and response window.
5. Resolve the switch.
6. Activate the frequent-sensing rail and its decision loop.
7. Activate the monthly-planning rail and its measured calendar loop.
8. Activate the special-method siding.
9. Clear every accent and hold the complete still.

## 6. Hard QA Risks

- no raw crop rectangles floating above the source
- no highlight crossing into a neighboring route or label
- no logo movement or glow
- no text movement, erasure, or reconstruction
- no route signal that suggests sensing is always superior
- no simultaneous distant motion that turns the artifact into visual noise
- first and final lossless frames must be pixel-identical to `visual.png`

## 7. Motion Treatments

All treatments use the same immutable `visual.png`, registered source-derived highlights, 9-second duration, and motion-free final hold.

| Option | Composition | Motion grammar | Best use |

The treatments are not palette swaps. They vary what motion communicates while keeping text, logos, and generated geometry locked.
