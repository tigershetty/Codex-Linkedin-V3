# EOQ Motion Frame

## Source of truth

- The approved `assets/visual.png` is immutable artwork.
- Canvas: 1003 x 1568 portrait.
- Preserve every word, number, logo, formula, panel edge, shadow, and object.

## Motion character

- Executive, deliberate, and useful.
- One decision signal moves through the operating logic.
- Motion illuminates existing geometry; it does not redraw or replace it.
- Build, breathe, resolve. First and final frames are the untouched visual.

## Brand behavior

- Cobalt is the primary motion ink.
- Red and green appear only where they already encode trade-offs or verification.
- No broad white sweep, floating UI card, extra label, decorative particle field, or camera shake.
- No movement, blur, or glow on body text or logos.

## Compositing rules

- No duplicated raw rectangular crops from the approved artwork.
- Flat color elements use semantic alpha masks derived from source pixels.
- Compound object cards use source-fitted clean plates during the reset.
- Integrated charts or 3D scenes reveal as whole semantic components when their shadows cannot be separated cleanly.
- All motion remains clipped to its semantic component.
- SVG tracks may only follow established curves, connectors, or geometric panel boundaries.
- At rest, disabling overlays must reproduce the source PNG exactly.

## Delayed-reveal decision

- Start on the complete approved visual.
- Clear only the formula, cost chart, batch models, trade-off indicators, and verification checks.
- Rebuild those components in the board's actual reading order.
- Keep the heading, input labels, explanatory text, logos, panel frames, and factory scene locked.
- Hold the complete visual without accents before the loop closes.
