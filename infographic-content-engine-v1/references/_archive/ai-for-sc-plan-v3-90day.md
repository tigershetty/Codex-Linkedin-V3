# Shetty's Desk — AI for Supply Chain: 90-Day Content Plan
**Version**: 3
**Used by**: `/ai-for-sc` skill (topic lookup)
**Period**: April – July 2026
**Format**: 24 single-image infographics, 2 per week, 12 weeks
**Series hashtag**: #AIforSupplyChain (used in captions, not on infographic)

---

## Series Philosophy

Every post title is a supply chain problem. AI is the method inside, not the headline. The person scrolling LinkedIn should think *that is MY problem* before they think *this uses AI*. The engagement trigger is professional identity, not technology curiosity.

Role-specific prompts go deep, not wide. The 10 prompts for demand planners are not "ask AI to analyse your data." They are "decompose my demand into trend, seasonality, and noise, then tell me which component drove last month's error." Each prompt targets a specific lever that separates a competent practitioner from an extraordinary one.

**A/B/C engagement mix** runs through every month:
- **Type A — Cheat Sheet**: drives saves and shares (prompt lists, reference cards)
- **Type B — Workflow**: gets shared by managers to teams (step-by-step with sample prompts)
- **Type C — Conversation Starter**: drives comments and organic reach (bold opinion, career/mindset)

**Tool approach**: free-first. Only 3.3% of M365 seats have Copilot. All prompts work with ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini free tiers. Copilot noted as bonus where relevant.

## Prompt Content Reference

The full prompt text for each Cheat Sheet (Type A) topic is stored separately to keep this plan scannable. When generating content for a Type A topic, the `/ai-for-sc` skill expands the prompts from the topic's row in the docx source document. The key prompts per topic:

### Topic 1 — Demand Planning (10 prompts)
1. Decompose demand into trend, seasonality, and noise — which component drove last month's forecast error?
2. Identify systematic bias by product family — model bias or input bias?
3. Compare 3 forecasting methods for THIS demand pattern and show accuracy trade-offs
4. List 5 leading indicators for [my industry] that could improve forecast 8 weeks ahead
5. Detect demand outliers to exclude from baseline — explain why each is an outlier
6. Calculate forecast value-add: adjusted forecast vs. naive statistical baseline
7. Analyse promotion lift history — uplifts consistently over- or under-estimated?
8. Write S&OP demand review narrative with root causes for top 3 variance drivers
9. Which SKUs benefit from demand sensing vs. monthly statistical forecasting?
10. Calculate revenue at risk from current forecast accuracy — what would a 5-point MAPE improvement be worth?

### Topic 3 — Supply Planning (10 prompts)
1. Audit safety stock parameters — which SKUs not recalculated in 6+ months?
2. Model primary supplier lead time doubling for 8 weeks — where do I stock out first?
3. Calculate cost of current safety stock vs. cost of stockouts it prevents
4. Which SKUs should shift from MTS to MTO based on demand variability and margin?
5. Build supplier flexibility matrix: which absorb 20% volume swing, which cannot?
6. Compare 3 replenishment strategies for top 50 SKUs: fixed order qty, fixed interval, min-max
7. Stress-test: demand spikes 25% while two suppliers go on allocation simultaneously
8. Identify bottleneck for Product Family X — supplier capacity, production throughput, or warehouse space?
9. Calculate working capital impact of reducing inventory by 5 days
10. Compare peak season options: overtime vs. additional shift vs. outsourcing

### Topic 7 — Production Planning (5 prompts)
1. Suggest 3 production sequences minimising changeover time while meeting deadlines
2. Calculate optimal batch size given setup time, daily demand, and holding cost
3. Identify bottleneck by cycle time per station — cost per hour of idle constraint
4. Model 3 capacity scenarios: overtime, weekend shift, outsource to co-packer
5. OEE dropped from 78% to 65% — categorise losses by availability, performance, quality

### Topic 9 — Procurement (10 prompts)
1. Build TCO comparison for 3 supplier quotes (unit price + 6 cost layers)
2. Analyse 3-year category spend for consolidation opportunities
3. Research current market pricing trends for [commodity] — 3 data points to challenge supplier
4. Summarise 50-page supplier contract, flag 5 highest-risk clauses
5. Supplier's 3 most likely arguments for 12% increase — draft rebuttals
6. Generate supplier evaluation scorecard for QBRs
7. Draft RFP with evaluation criteria, weighted scoring, timeline
8. Analyse maverick spend — buying outside contracts, estimated cost leakage
9. Supplier risk profile: single/dual source, geographic concentration, financial health, contract expiry
10. Working capital benefit of extending payment terms 30 to 60 days across top 20 suppliers

### Topic 11 — Logistics (5 prompts)
1. Compare 3 carrier quotes by total transport cost (base + fuel + accessorials + delay cost)
2. Draft exception report: root cause, customer impact, corrective + preventive action
3. Summarise customs/import requirements for [product] from [origin] to [destination] including recent tariff changes
4. Build carrier performance dashboard: OTIF%, cost/kg, transit time, claims rate, invoice accuracy
5. Analyse 6-month transport spend — 3 lanes overpaying or where consolidation reduces frequency

### Topic 19 — Warehouse (5 prompts)
1. Analyse warehouse utilisation by zone — slow-movers in prime picking, fast-movers in poor access
2. Build optimised receiving schedule balancing dock capacity across the week
3. Slotting strategy: golden zone, bulk reserve, offsite/discontinuation candidates
4. Throughput rate per labour hour by shift — underperforming shifts and likely causes
5. Model space impact of 500 new SKUs — what moves, what consolidates, expansion cost vs. slotting cost

### Topic 23 — Business Case (5 prompts)
1. Calculate annualised cost of the current problem
2. Model expected improvement — cost avoidance, cost reduction, working capital improvement
3. Payback analysis — month-by-month cumulative savings vs. investment
4. Top 3 risks of NOT doing this project — cost of inaction
5. One-page executive summary: problem, solution, investment, return, payback, risk of inaction
