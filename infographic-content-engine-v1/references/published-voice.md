## Tiger's Post Structure (7 parts)

Every published deep dive follows this structure. The structure is a guide, not a straitjacket. Vary how each part is expressed so posts do not all read identically.

1. **Hook** (1–2 sentences) — use Selected Hook from message-commit.md verbatim
2. **Context** (1–2 sentences) — mechanism or cost of inaction; does NOT reveal anchor case result yet
3. **Three named bullets** — narrative format, numbers embedded naturally. Vary bullet structure across the three: not all should follow the same bold-label-plus-explanation format. One might be a question, one might lead with the number, one might build through reasoning.
4. **Opinion** — use Tiger's natural opinion bridges: "My view on this is that...", "If you look at the bigger picture...", "Here's the thing most people miss...". Rotate between posts. Do NOT default to "My take:" every time.
5. **CTA question** — single open question, ≥15 words, names "your organisation" or "your supply chain". Be specific enough that the reader knows exactly what to examine.
6. **Save Hook** — OPTIONAL; only when topic has genuinely reusable benchmarks; write naturally
7. **Hashtags** — #ShettysDesk #SupplyChainIntelligence #SCM + 2 topic-specific tags (5 max)

**Word count**: 200–350 words total. Never exceed 350.

## Voice Rules (from tiger-voice.md — applied to deep dive)

### Rhythm and Flow
- Write sentences that connect ideas with reasoning: "because", "so", "which means", "and then"
- Vary sentence length: mix longer flowing sentences with occasional shorter ones for emphasis. The default is flowing, not staccato.
- Build through ideas rather than listing them. Add reasoning as you go.
- Allow natural imperfections. Not every sentence needs to be grammatically pristine.

### Opinion Style
- Do NOT use "My take:" as the default opinion marker
- Use Tiger's natural bridges and rotate between posts:
  - "My view on this is that..."
  - "My personal reflection has been that..."
  - "If I were in your shoes..."
  - "If you look at the bigger picture..."
  - "Here's the thing most people miss..."

### Pattern Breakers (use at least 1 per post)
- Start a sentence with "And" or "But"
- Include one specific detail from real experience (a real meeting, a real product, a real number from Tiger's work)
- Use a parenthetical aside mid-sentence (signals thinking in real time)
- Vary bullet structure across the three bullets
- Let a sentence run longer than feels "optimal" when working through an idea

### Hard Rules
See `tiger-voice.md` — Hard Rules section. Non-negotiable across all pipelines.

## Voice Markers (preserve these patterns)

These phrases and structures appear across published posts. Use them as models, not as templates to repeat verbatim:

- **"X is breaking that mold"** — signals a company doing something unexpected in the space
- **"It is a gamble on [X] instead of just [Y]"** — frames a strategic bet with an embedded contrast
- **Emphasis through a single shorter sentence after a longer one** — "That sequence matters more than anything." Earns its place by contrast, not by default.
- **Personal frame as opener**: "I've been dissecting..." — signals depth of analysis, not surface commentary
- **Named firms in bullets**: "What KPMG, Deloitte, and Philips found when they looked closely:" — authority without formality
- **Numbers embedded mid-sentence**: "...cut lead times from 3 months to 2 weeks, not by hiring faster designers..." — the number lands harder mid-thought than at the start

## What Tiger's Voice is NOT

Do NOT write in the following ways. These are common AI defaults that do not match the published voice:

```
NOT THIS (ANCHORS slot format):
A (Hook): Nike's DTC reversal raises questions.
N (Bridge): The shift from wholesale to direct...
H (Insights):
  - Nike lost 50% stock value. (Nike, 2024.)
O (Opinion): My read: The DTC bet failed.

NOT THIS (McKinsey formality):
"The strategic implications of Nike's distribution channel reconfiguration
suggest a fundamental reassessment of the direct-to-consumer model..."

NOT THIS (citation format in bullets):
• Nike experienced a 50-60% stock price decline. (Nike Inc., Fiscal 2024.)
• Morgan Stanley noted channel inventory excess. (Morgan Stanley, 2024.)

NOT THIS (choppy AI fragments):
"Supply chain sees the globe. Logistics sees the port. Not the same role."
"One designs. The other delivers."
"Short. Punchy. Done."

NOT THIS (em dash):
"not better technology — it is process design"
Write instead: "not better technology. It is process design."

NOT THIS (AI slop language):
"leverage", "utilize", "delve into", "moreover", "furthermore",
"it is important to note", "one might consider", "in today's landscape",
"it could be argued", "plays a crucial role", "navigating the complexities of"
```

**Writing rule**: Tiger writes as he speaks. Sentences flow and connect with reasoning. Numbers and organisations are named inside sentences, not appended as citations. Every word earns its place, but the default is connected thought, not isolated fragments.
