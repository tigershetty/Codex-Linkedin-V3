## 1. ADOPTION STATISTICS — Supply Chain Specific

### Gartner (June 2025) — 120 SC leaders surveyed
- **Only 23% of supply chain organizations have a formal AI strategy**
- 77% have no structured AI approach despite widespread experimentation
- CSCOs feel pressure to achieve short-term ROI, risking long-term AI transformation
- Source: [Gartner Press Release](https://www.gartner.com/en/newsroom/2025-06-11-gartner-survey-shows-just-23-percent-of-supply-chain-organizations-have-a-formal-ai-strategy)

### Gartner (September 2025)
- **70% of large organizations will adopt AI-based SC forecasting by 2030** — meaning 30% still won't even by then
- Source: [Gartner Press Release](https://www.gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2025-09-16-gartner-predicts-70-percent-of-large-orgs-will-adopt-ai-based-supply-chain-forecasting-to-predict-future-demand-by-2030)

### Gartner (February 2026) — 509 SC leaders
- 55% of SC leaders expect agentic AI to reduce entry-level hiring needs
- 51% believe agentic AI will drive overall workforce reductions
- 86% agree agentic AI adoption will require new talent development processes
- Source: [Gartner Press Release](https://www.gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2026-02-25-gartner-survey-shows-55-percent-of-supply-chain-leaders-expect-agentic-ai-to-reduce-entry-level-hiring-needs)

### BCG (September 2025) — Logistics specific
- **Only 10% of logistics companies are fully embracing generative AI**
- Early adopters gaining a decisive competitive edge
- Source: [BCG — Agentic AI in Logistics](https://www.bcg.com/publications/2025/ai-in-logistics-a-strategic-imperative)

### BCG (2026) — Supply Chain Planning
- **88% of organizations use AI in some capacity, but only 39% report measurable EBIT impact**
- **95% of enterprise AI pilots deliver zero measurable return** (MIT NANDA study, July 2025)
- Technology-first approaches see single-digit productivity improvements; some see negative returns after implementation costs
- **60% of SC digital adoption efforts will fail to deliver promised value by 2028** (Gartner)
- Source: [BCG — SC Planning 2026](https://www.bcg.com/publications/2026/supply-chain-planning-why-ai-alone-isnt-enough)

### ProcureAbility (January 2026) — Procurement specific
- **100% of procurement leaders report some AI utilization** — but only **11% are "fully ready"**
- 65% consider themselves "mostly ready" (clear strategy, active pilots)
- 89% not fully ready to implement AI at scale
- Top 3 barriers: data privacy/compliance (67%), insufficient data quality (54%), fears of AI replacing human judgment (51%)
- Source: [ProcureAbility 2026 CPO Report](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/procureabilitys-2026-cpo-report-reveals-the-top-barriers-to-ai-adoption-among-procurement-organizations-302666226.html)

### ABI Research (2025) — 490 SC professionals surveyed
- 64% say AI/Gen AI capabilities are important when evaluating new technology
- 94% plan to use AI for decision support within 2 years
- 91% plan to use AI for demand forecasting
- 76% see potential for autonomous AI agents (reordering, rerouting)
- **80% of SC leaders plan AI deployment** — but "plan" ≠ "doing"
- Source: [ABI Research](https://www.abiresearch.com/blog/artificial-intelligence-ai-in-supply-chain-survey-results)

### Caption-ready stat combinations:
- "23% of supply chain organizations have a formal AI strategy. 88% say they use AI. That 65-point gap is the real story."
- "100% of procurement leaders report using AI. 11% are actually ready. The gap between touching and using is enormous."
- "Only 10% of logistics companies fully embrace generative AI. 90% are watching."

## 2. THE ACTIVATION GAP — Awareness vs. Usage

### PwC (November 2025) — 49,843 workers, 48 countries, 28 sectors
- **54% have used AI for work in the past 12 months**
- **Only 14% use GenAI daily** (up from 12% in 2024 — barely moved)
- Only 6% use agentic AI daily
- Daily users report: 92% productivity improvement vs. 58% for infrequent users
- Source: [PwC 2025 Global Workforce Survey](https://www.pwc.com/gx/en/news-room/press-releases/2025/pwc-2025-global-workforce-survey.html)

### Microsoft Work Trend Index (April 2025)
- **75% of knowledge workers use AI at work** — but 46% started less than 6 months ago
- **82% of leaders say AI skills are essential — but 60% of employees say they lack them**
- **Only 39% of people who use AI at work have received AI training from their company**
- Source: [Microsoft Work Trend Index](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/worklab/work-trend-index/ai-at-work-is-here-now-comes-the-hard-part)

### ActivTrak (March 2026) — 443 million hours of work activity, 163,638 employees, 1,111 companies
- **80% of employees now use AI tools** (up from 53% two years ago)
- **But 57% spend less than 1% of their total work hours in AI tools**
- Only 3% of employees hit the productivity sweet spot (7-10% of work hours in AI)
- 92% monthly usage retention — adoption is sticky, not experimental
- Source: [ActivTrak 2026 State of the Workplace](https://www.activtrak.com/blog/2026-state-of-the-workplace/)

### Worklytics (2025) — Daily usage by department
- **Operations: 30-45% daily usage** (advanced orgs: 50-55%)
- Engineering: 85-90%, Marketing: 55-70%, Sales: 45-60%, HR: 35-50%
- Operations is the lowest adoption department tracked
- Source: [Worklytics 2025 Benchmarks](https://www.worklytics.co/resources/2025-employee-generative-ai-usage-benchmarks-daily-adoption-rates)

### Caption-ready stat combinations:
- "80% of employees have used AI. 57% spend less than 1% of their work time in it. That's not adoption. That's tourism."
- "Operations has the lowest daily AI usage of any department: 30-45%. Engineering is at 85-90%. Same tools. Same access. Different activation."
- "75% of knowledge workers use AI. Only 14% use it daily. The gap between tried-it and use-it is 61 percentage points."

## 3. FIRST-PROMPT BARRIERS — Why SC Professionals Don't Use AI

### BCG AI at Work (June 2025) — 13,000+ employees
- **48% of employees rank training as the most important factor for AI adoption**
- **Nearly half report receiving minimal or no training**
- 18% of regular AI users say they received no training at all
- When employees lack AI tools, **54% say they'll find and use alternatives anyway**
- Frontline employees hit a "silicon ceiling" — only 51% regularly use AI vs. 76%+ of leaders/managers
- 79% who received 5+ hours training are regular users vs. 67% with less
- Source: [BCG AI at Work 2025](https://www.bcg.com/publications/2025/ai-at-work-momentum-builds-but-gaps-remain)

### McKinsey Superagency (January 2025)
- **Employees are 3x more likely than leaders expect to be using Gen AI for 30%+ of daily work**
- 92% of companies plan to increase AI investment — but **only 1% report AI maturity**
- 47% of C-suite leaders say their companies are moving too slowly on AI
- 51% of employees cite cybersecurity as their top AI concern
- Source: [McKinsey Superagency](https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/tech-and-ai/our-insights/superagency-in-the-workplace-empowering-people-to-unlock-ais-full-potential-at-work)

### Deloitte State of AI in Enterprise (2025-2026) — 3,235 leaders
- **Data security (67%) and data quality (65%)** are the top barriers
- Regulatory compliance rose from 28% to 38% as a barrier
- Insufficient worker skills seen as biggest barrier to integrating AI into workflows
- 69% of organizations expect 1+ year to implement AI governance
- Source: [Deloitte State of AI](https://www.deloitte.com/us/en/what-we-do/capabilities/applied-artificial-intelligence/content/state-of-ai-in-the-enterprise.html)

### Supply Chain-Specific Barriers (SupplyChainBrain)
1. Lack of big, clean data
2. Compartmentalized AI (fragmented across functions)
3. Black box vs. explainable AI (trust issues)
4. Short-sighted optimization (re-planning disruption)
5. Over-enthusiastic AI vendors (unclear terminology)
6. AI skills gap (largest barrier per O'Reilly)
- Source: [SupplyChainBrain](https://www.supplychainbrain.com/blogs/1-think-tank/post/30051-six-barriers-to-getting-results-with-ai-in-supply-chain-management)

### Barrier synthesis for SC professionals (individual-level, not enterprise):
1. **"I don't know what to type"** — No training (48% say it's critical, ~50% get none), no prompt examples in SC context
2. **"I'm worried about data confidentiality"** — 67% cite data security, 40% cite privacy (Deloitte); procurement: 67% cite privacy/compliance (ProcureAbility)
3. **"I don't see how it applies to my role"** — Operations has lowest daily AI usage (30-45%); SC-specific use cases barely covered in training
4. **"I don't have time to experiment"** — 68% of employees struggle with work pace and volume (Microsoft); 35% feel overwhelmed weekly (PwC)
5. **"My company hasn't said anything about AI policy"** — 63% of companies lack a generative AI usage policy; 2 in 5 workers say no guidelines exist
6. **"I'll get in trouble if I try"** — 51% of procurement leaders fear AI replacing human judgment; cybersecurity is top employee concern (51%, McKinsey)

## 4. FIRST STEPS IN PRACTICE — How SC Professionals Actually Started

### Common first use cases (from practitioner accounts and case studies):
- **Meeting prep and S&OP narratives** — teams use ChatGPT to prep for meetings, think through risks, explain data
- **Exception reports and customer communication** — logistics coordinators drafting delay explanations
- **Data analysis and pattern recognition** — demand planners feeding forecast vs. actuals data
- **Supplier risk and contract review** — procurement uploading contracts for clause flagging
- Source: [Francesca Tabor — ChatGPT Use Cases for Manufacturing & SC](https://www.francescatabor.com/articles/2025/12/5/top-chat-gpt-use-cases-for-manufacturing-amp-supply-chain)

### What works vs. what doesn't:
- **Works**: Giving detailed context ("We're holding 45 days of safety stock, lead times just jumped from 8 to 12 weeks") vs. vague ("help with inventory")
- **Doesn't work**: Expecting AI to replace domain knowledge; using it without understanding what good output looks like
- **94% of procurement executives use generative AI weekly** (AI at Wharton, 2025) — up 44 percentage points YoY
- Bottom-up adoption: individual buyers already using ChatGPT even when the enterprise doesn't endorse it
- Source: [BCG GenAI in Procurement](https://www.bcg.com/publications/2025/from-buzz-to-bottom-line-cost-reductions-using-genai)

## 5. THE COPILOT GAP — Enterprise AI Access

### Microsoft Copilot Adoption (2026)
- **15 million paid Copilot seats** out of 450 million M365 commercial subscribers = **3.3% penetration**
- Up 160% year-over-year — growing fast but from tiny base
- **Workplace conversion rate: only 35.8%** of those with access actively use it
- **When employees have both Copilot and ChatGPT access, 76% choose ChatGPT**, 18% choose Copilot
- Copilot market share fell from 18.8% (July 2025) to 11.5% (January 2026)
- ChatGPT holds 55.2% market share among paid AI subscribers
- Source: [Stackmatix Copilot Statistics](https://www.stackmatix.com/blog/copilot-market-adoption-trends), [AI Business Weekly](https://aibusinessweekly.net/p/microsoft-copilot-statistics)

### What this means for SC professionals:
- 96.7% of M365 users do NOT have Copilot
- Even those who do — only 1 in 3 actually uses it
- The average SC professional's AI access is a free ChatGPT/Claude/Gemini account, not enterprise tools
- Free-tier tools are the actual on-ramp — not company-provided solutions

### Caption-ready stat:
- "3.3% of M365 seats have Copilot. That means 96.7% of the people in your company don't have enterprise AI. The free tier IS the enterprise tier for most supply chain professionals."

## 6. COMPANY POLICY LANDSCAPE

### Policy gaps:
- **63% of companies lack a generative AI usage policy** (Apollo Technical, 2026)
- **Nearly 2 in 5 workers (40%) report their company has no AI usage guidelines** (BlackFog)
- 69% of organizations expect 1+ year to implement AI governance strategies (Deloitte)
- Source: [Apollo Technical](https://www.apollotechnical.com/surprising-statistics-on-ai-in-the-workplace/)

### The barrier is ambiguity, not prohibition:
- Most companies haven't said "no" — they've said nothing
- Employees fill the vacuum: 54% say they'd use AI tools even without approval (BCG)
- The policy silence creates uncertainty that stops risk-averse professionals (like those in supply chain operations) from starting

## 7. PERMISSION VS. FORGIVENESS

### Fishbowl Survey (January 2023) — 11,793 professionals
- **43% of professionals have used AI tools for work**
- **68% did NOT tell their boss** (70% in some reporting)
- Only 32% disclosed their AI usage to supervisors
- Respondents from Amazon, Bank of America, Google, IBM, JP Morgan, McKinsey, Meta, Nike
- Source: [Fishbowl](https://www.fishbowlapp.com/insights/70-percent-of-workers-using-chatgpt-at-work-are-not-telling-their-boss/)

### Shadow AI (2025):
- **49% of workers use AI tools without employer approval** (BlackFog)
- **59% of US employees use unapproved AI tools** (Cybernews)
- **38% share sensitive work data with AI without permission**
- **69% of C-suite and 66% of senior VPs are okay with this** — executives prioritize speed over process
- Executives have the highest shadow AI usage of any level
- 90% of organizations claim confidence in AI defenses, but 68% report actual data leaks from staff feeding info into AI; only 23% have serious policies
- Sources: [CIO](https://www.cio.com/article/4124760/roughly-half-of-employees-are-using-unsanctioned-ai-tools-and-enterprise-leaders-are-major-culprits.html), [Cybernews](https://cybernews.com/ai-news/ai-shadow-use-workplace-survey/)

### Caption-ready stat:
- "68% of employees using AI at work didn't tell their boss. The biggest AI adoption barrier isn't technology. It's the conversation nobody is having."

## 8. TIME-TO-VALUE — How Quickly SC Professionals See Results

### Individual-level gains (days/weeks):
- **10-25% performance boost** in typical knowledge tasks (writing, research, analysis) — measurable within days of proper use (MIT studies)
- **14-15% productivity gain** in structured environments like call centers (measured per hour)
- **40% performance boost potential** for workers using AI (MIT researchers, broader study)
- Source: [Fortune — AI Productivity Paradox](https://fortune.com/2026/02/17/ai-productivity-paradox-ceo-study-robert-solow-information-technology-age/)

### But the macro picture is sobering:
- **CEO survey of 6,000 executives**: average AI usage is only 1.5 hours per week; 25% report zero workplace AI usage
- **Nearly 90% of executives said AI had no impact on employment or productivity** over past 3 years
- Companies invested **$250+ billion in AI during 2024** with minimal documented productivity returns
- Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis: only **1.9% excess productivity growth** since ChatGPT's launch
- Source: [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/02/17/ai-productivity-paradox-ceo-study-robert-solow-information-technology-age/)

### The "first win" pattern:
- PwC: daily AI users are **far more optimistic** about role futures (69% vs. 44% for non-users)
- Daily users saw **salary increases at 52%** vs. 32% for infrequent users
- Daily users report **improved job security at 58%** vs. 36% for infrequent users
- BCG: employees who receive **5+ hours of training** — 79% become regular users vs. 67% with less
- Source: [PwC 2025 Global Workforce Survey](https://www.pwc.com/gx/en/news-room/press-releases/2025/pwc-2025-global-workforce-survey.html)

### The sweet spot (ActivTrak, actual behavioral data):
- Employees spending **7-10% of work hours in AI** have the highest productivity (95% productivity rate)
- For a 40-hour week, that's **2.8 to 4 hours per week** — not all-day usage, not five-minute dabbling
- Only **3% of employees** have found this sweet spot
- Source: [ActivTrak](https://www.activtrak.com/blog/2026-state-of-the-workplace/)

### Caption-ready stat:
- "The AI productivity sweet spot is 3-4 hours per week. Not all day. Not never. 3% of employees have found it. The other 97% are either overusing, underusing, or not using at all."
