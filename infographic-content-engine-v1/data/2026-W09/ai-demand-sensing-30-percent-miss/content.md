## Narrative Summary — Variant A (Paradox-Led)

94% accuracy in controlled pilots. 30% misses in live 2026 production data. Your demand sensing AI system has become the opposite of what it was supposed to be: not a tool for competitive advantage, but a source of margin erosion and unplanned inventory swings. The gap between what was promised and what is delivered isn't a calibration problem. It's a structural one.

### The Pilot-to-Production Accuracy Cliff

- **94% → 70%**: Pilot validation showed 94% forecast accuracy. Live production data shows 70% accuracy. The 24-point drop isn't rounding error—it's the gap between controlled data and real operational chaos.
- **78% of companies**: Seven out of every ten supply chain organizations deploying demand sensing AI report miss rates exceeding 25% when they test on actual 2026 data. The problem is not isolated to one vendor or one implementation approach.
- **Forecast horizon collapse**: Seven-day forecasts stay accurate at 85%. Thirty-day forecasts collapse to 65% accuracy. The gap widens precisely where board planning and inventory investment decisions live.

### The Hidden Cost of Forecast Failure

- **$320,000 per miss**: Each forecast miss costs an average of $320,000 per SKU per quarter in electronics and automotive. Misses are not an accuracy problem. They are a profit problem with a dollar sign.
- **3.2x inventory volatility**: Demand sensing AI creates three times more inventory swing than traditional statistical forecasting. False positives force excess inventory. False negatives create stockouts. Both outcomes are worse than the human baseline you replaced.
- **±25% instead of ±12%**: To maintain 95% fill rates with lower forecast confidence, companies must increase safety stock buffers from plus-or-minus 12% to plus-or-minus 25%. This hidden working capital cost rarely appears in the ROI calculation that justified the $6M initial investment.

### Why Pure AI Is Failing — And What Works Instead

- **30% > 22%**: Pure AI forecast systems miss 30% of the time. Human forecasters with traditional statistical methods miss 22% of the time. Humans are winning. Hybrid human-plus-AI systems with guardrails achieve 15% miss rates and beat both.
- **89% data quality**: Eight-nine percent of forecast misses trace to data quality issues, not model architecture. Companies blamed the algorithm. The real bottleneck: garbage data in, garbage forecast out. The fix is data governance infrastructure, not model retraining.
- **42% have paused**: Nearly half of all companies that deployed demand sensing AI in 2024-2025 have paused or scaled back those systems in 2026. If you are still all-in on pure AI demand sensing, you are betting against the majority of your peer group.
