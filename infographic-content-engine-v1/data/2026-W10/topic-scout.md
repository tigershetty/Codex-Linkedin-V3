Section 122 tariffs (effective February 24) are simultaneously forcing cargo front-loading decisions using ocean routes that now carry 40% higher insurance premiums. 2026-W10 is a compound disruption week — the highest concentration of simultaneously active supply chain risk signals since Liberation Day tariffs in 2025-W09.
