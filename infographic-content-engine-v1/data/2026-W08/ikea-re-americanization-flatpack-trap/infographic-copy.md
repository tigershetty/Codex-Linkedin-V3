  BUT the same scale dependency makes US domestic production ~40% more expensive per unit,
  creating a tariff trap where absorption is unsustainable but full localization is uneconomic.

wrong_assumption:
  "Flat-pack design insulates us from tariff exposure — we just need to keep optimizing imports."

  €1.7B operating profit compressed in FY25 with tariff costs "partly absorbed" (management quote);
  prices raised $50 per sofa and $100 per bedroom set in October 2025;
  40% effective tariff rate on 85% of US-sold products with no ceiling before July 24, 2026.

decision_shift:
  Segment your SKU portfolio by flat-pack shipping efficiency ratio. Identify which categories
  cross the domestic production break-even at current tariff rates. Start with bulky, heavy,
  low-efficiency items (cabinets done; mattresses and sofas next). Lock domestic commitments
  before Section 122 expires July 24, 2026.

mechanism_chain:
  trigger:               IEEPA struck unconstitutional (Feb 20, 2026) + Section 122 15% surcharge

  economic_consequence:  €1.7B FY25 operating profit (compressed), prices raised $50-$100,

  decision_implication:  Calculate domestic break-even by category; bulky items cross threshold
