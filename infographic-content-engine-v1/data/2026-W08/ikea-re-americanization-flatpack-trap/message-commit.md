## Committed Message
The same flat-pack design that made IKEA the world's most efficient shipper has made domestic manufacturing in America structurally impossible at any plausible tariff rate.

## Hero Number
**Figure**: 10x
**What it proves**: flat-pack container density makes US scale impossible

## Opening Sentences

**Variant A** (paradox-first — used to open Variant A in content.md):
IKEA built EUR 44.6 billion in global sales on one design insight: flat-pack furniture ships 10 times more product per container than assembled rivals. The same design now makes domestic manufacturing in America structurally impossible at scale. The moat and the trap are the same mechanism.

**Variant B** (scene-first — used to open Variant B in content.md):
